A week ago the Drake-and-Ella story was two words in an Instagram comment. Now it's Drake standing in an Arkansas arena crowd, singing every word of "Choosin' Texas." Things move fast when the biggest name in music decides he's paying attention.

Here's what happened. On Saturday (Aug. 22), Ella played Simmons Bank Arena in North Little Rock, and partway through the set the big screens cut to Drake in the crowd, grinning and singing along to ["Choosin' Texas"](/songs/choosin-texas). The room, understandably, lost it. This comes just days after [Drake called Ella a "goated pen" on Instagram](/news/2026-08-20-drake-ella-langley-goated-pen) — the highest compliment one songwriter can hand another — so the two of them circling each other is no longer a one-off.

What's got fans really talking is who was standing next to Drake: ERNEST, one of the openers on the Dandelion Tour. If you follow the wiring behind country's biggest crossovers, that detail matters. ERNEST started out rapping, became one of Nashville's most in-demand writers, co-writes constantly with Ella, and is one of Morgan Wallen's closest collaborators. In other words, if a country-rap song were going to get sketched out, this is exactly the room where it would happen.

The timing adds to the intrigue. Drake has been teasing what looks like his next project, reportedly titled *Fear of Missing Out*, sharing a photo of a CD sitting on the same table where he first revealed the names of his last three albums. Nobody has confirmed Ella is on it, and we want to be clear about that — this is fan speculation and industry tea-reading, not an announcement. But Drake is famous for rollouts where every "random" move turns out to be planned, and a surprise pop-up at a country show in a city with no obvious connection to him has people convinced it wasn't an accident.

Could it be nothing more than a superstar enjoying a great live set? Sure. Drake has name-dropped country artists for years and has been spinning "Choosin' Texas" in his DJ sets for months. But a comment, a concert cameo, and a mystery project all inside one week is the kind of run that usually ends with a link in someone's bio.
