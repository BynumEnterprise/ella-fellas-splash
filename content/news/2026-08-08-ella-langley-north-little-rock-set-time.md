Ella Langley brings The Dandelion Tour to **Simmons Bank Arena in North Little Rock on Saturday, August 22, 2026** — the closing night of her late-August arena swing before the tour rolls into the fall. It's a big room, the bill runs three deep, and if you're driving in from around Little Rock the main question is simple: what time does she actually go on? Here's the timing and the plan.

## Who plays when

Simmons Bank Arena has posted the two numbers that matter, and they're the ones to build your night around:

- **7:00 PM CT** — show starts (the venue's own posted time)
- **6:00 PM CT** — doors open

The bill, per the venue and the Arkansas Democrat-Gazette announcement, is **Laci Kaye Booth** first, then **ERNEST** in the direct-support slot, then **Ella Langley**.

We're not going to make up a minute for when Ella walks out. The arena has published the show time and doors but not a per-act running order, so 7:00 PM is the honest, sourced answer — the first opener goes on right around the time printed on your ticket, and on recent three-act Dandelion nights Ella has closed the evening after both openers wrap. If Simmons Bank Arena posts a full schedule closer to the date, this page and the [set-times page](/tour/north-little-rock-simmons-bank-arena-2026-08-22/set-times) will get the real times.

Get there early enough for the openers. ERNEST isn't a name to skip — he's one of Nashville's most-cut songwriters with a hit run of his own, and putting him in the direct-support spot means the room will already be loud by the time Ella takes over. Laci Kaye Booth opens the night and is worth catching from the first song.

## Simmons Bank Arena notes

This is an easy one to plan for on the comfort front: indoor arena, climate controlled, no weather to sweat. It sits right on the North Little Rock riverfront at 1 Simmons Bank Arena Drive. The venue runs a **clear-bag policy** and is **cashless**, so bring a card and leave the big tote at home to move through security faster. On-site lots run roughly **$15–$25**, and the Dickey-Stephens Park lots across the river are the usual overflow option if the arena lots fill.

One booking note worth knowing: the arena's premium **Legends Lounge passes are sold out**, and the general house is now resale-only (more on that below).

For where to sit — lower bowl versus the 200-level, and which sections get the best sound — see the [North Little Rock set-times and where-to-sit page](/tour/north-little-rock-simmons-bank-arena-2026-08-22/set-times).
