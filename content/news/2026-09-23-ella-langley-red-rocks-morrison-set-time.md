## Red Rocks notes: altitude, weather, and the climb

Red Rocks is an open-air amphitheatre carved into the rocks at about 6,450 feet, and early October evenings there can turn cold and windy fast once the sun drops behind the monoliths — think comfortable in the 60s at doors and possibly 40s by the encore. **Bring layers**, and check the forecast the day of; a rain shell is smart, but note that umbrellas are not permitted, so pack a poncho instead of one. The seating is stone bench rows, so a stadium cushion earns its keep, and the altitude is real if you're coming from sea level — hydrate and take the stairs at your own pace.
