## Fair-show notes

This one's different from an arena, so a couple of things to sort before you go. First, it's **outdoors** — check the forecast, dress for a warm evening that can cool off after dark, and plan for sun while you're waiting for gates. Second, a Grandstand ticket and **fair admission are two separate things**; you'll want both to get to your seat. There's a bag policy at the gates, so travel light. And because it's a fairgrounds, give yourself extra time for parking and the walk in.

For where to sit — the Grandstand tiers run from the **Track** and premium **Blue Ribbon Zone** down through the reserved sections — see the [Springfield set-times and where-to-sit page](/tour/springfield-illinois-state-fair-2026-08-21/set-times).
