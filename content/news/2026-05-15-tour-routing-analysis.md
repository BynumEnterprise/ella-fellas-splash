*Correction (October 13, 2026): The original version of this post argued that the Dandelion Tour skipped the West Coast. It also predicted 2027 plans and cited tour figures we could not support. The tour did head west on its fall leg, so we've replaced the post with the actual dates.*

The Dandelion Tour opened May 7, 2026 in Toledo. Its spring and summer dates were mostly in the South, Midwest and East, which is where the "no West Coast" talk started. The fall leg went west:

- **Morrison, CO**: Red Rocks Amphitheatre, October 7, 2026 ([show page](/tour/morrison-red-rocks-amphitheatre-2026-10-07))
- **Lubbock, TX**: United Supermarkets Arena, October 10, 2026
- **Los Angeles, CA**: Greek Theatre, October 13 and 14, 2026 ([night one](/tour/los-angeles-greek-theatre-2026-10-13), [night two](/tour/los-angeles-greek-theatre-2026-10-14))
- **Salt Lake City, UT**: Redwest Festival at Utah State Fairpark, October 16, 2026 (festival date)

After that the tour returns to the Midwest. It finishes with Des Moines (October 29), Lincoln (October 30) and Saint Paul (October 31).

On Morgan Wallen's Still The Problem stadium tour, Ella also played direct support on select dates in 2026. Those dates and lineups are on our [Ella Langley + Morgan Wallen tour guide](/guides/ella-langley-morgan-wallen-2026-tour).

Already announced for 2027: Two Step Inn in Georgetown, TX (April 18) and Patriotic Festival in Virginia Beach (May 29). No 2027 headline tour has been announced. See our [2027 tour page](/guides/ella-langley-2027-tour) for updates.
