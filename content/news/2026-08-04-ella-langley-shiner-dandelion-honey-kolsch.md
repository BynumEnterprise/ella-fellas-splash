Ella Langley is getting her own beer. Shiner made it official Monday: the Texas brewery and Ella are launching a Dandelion Honey Kolsch, a signature brew named after the album that has owned our summer. Whiskey Riff and Billboard both carried the announcement, and the release date is one we already had circled for a very different reason — August 13, the same night [Ella headlines Moody Center in Austin](/tour/austin-moody-center-2026-08-13).

If you have watched the [Choosin' Texas](/songs/choosin-texas) video even once, you knew this was coming. Shiner Bock is practically a cast member in it. Holler reported that Shiner told distributors it expected that video to be the biggest beer placement in a music video all of 2026 — and then spent late July teasing this collaboration with a "Born to live free" video before confirming what everybody guessed.

## What the Dandelion Honey Kolsch actually is

Per Billboard, it is a kolsch-style beer brewed with dandelion honey — light, a little floral, the kind of thing you drink on an amphitheater lawn in August. Coverage of the announcement describes a label done in honey tones and covered in dandelions, pulling straight from the *Dandelion* album art. The album, for anyone keeping score at home, came out in April and debuted at number one on the Billboard 200, so naming a beer after it is not exactly a marketing risk.

This is Ella's first beer partnership, and it is a two-way street: she gets a Texas institution stamping her name on shelves, and Shiner gets the artist whose fan base — Holler wrote a whole piece on this — spans both the women who hear themselves in her songs and, well, us. The Ella Fellas got name-checked in their analysis of why this deal makes sense. We will take it.

## The launch party is in San Antonio on August 13

Per Whiskey Riff, the beer launches Thursday, August 13 with a free, denim-themed party at Otto's Ice House in San Antonio, running 6 to 9 PM — a DJ spinning Ella's catalog, local vendors, and a raffle. Nobody has billed Ella herself as appearing, which tracks, because she has an arena to play that night: Moody Center in Austin, about 80 miles up I-35, [doors and set times here](/tour/austin-moody-center-2026-08-13/set-times).

So if you are in central Texas that Thursday, you have a legitimately great problem. Hit the ice house early, raise a Dandelion Honey Kolsch, then decide whether you are staying for the raffle or pointing the truck north. (We know which one we would pick. The [full tour schedule](/tour) says she is in Corpus Christi the next night and Fort Worth the night after, so Texas has options all weekend.)

## Why this deal is smarter than a novelty can

Artist beer collabs usually feel bolted on. This one does not, and the reason is the song. [Choosin' Texas](/news/2026-07-22-choosin-texas-meaning) built its whole world around Texas iconography, Shiner included, months before any deal was announced — the brand was in the story before it was on the label. That is also why the timing works: the single crossed [15 weeks at number one on the Hot 100](/news/2026-07-28-ella-langley-choosin-texas-15-weeks-record) in late July, per Billboard, and the beer arrives the same week her Texas arena run begins.

Our advice: if you find it on a shelf, grab it — and if you are headed to one of the Texas shows, the [Amphitheater Lawn look](/shop/looks/amphitheater-lawn) pairs nicely with a cold one. We will report back on how it tastes.
