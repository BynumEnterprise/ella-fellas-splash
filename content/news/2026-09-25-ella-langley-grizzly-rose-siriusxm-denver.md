Every so often an artist this big does the exact opposite of what you'd expect, and Ella just did it. Instead of only playing the biggest rooms in the country this fall, she booked one of the smallest, and it's the kind of night most of us would trade a fistful of arena tickets for. Ella Langley is bringing SiriusXM's Small Stage Series to The Grizzly Rose in Denver on Tuesday, October 6, an intimate show inside one of Colorado's most legendary honky-tonks.

Here's the part that makes this one worth acting on today rather than filing away: you can't just buy a ticket, and the window to get in is closing fast.

## What's happening at the Grizzly Rose

Per SiriusXM's announcement, Ella will play the Small Stage Series, presented by Reese's, at The Grizzly Rose on October 6, running through her hits, fan favorites, and songs from the Dandelion album. The Small Stage Series is exactly what it sounds like: a stadium-level star in a room that holds a sliver of a normal crowd. For a tour that's been selling out 19,000-seat arenas all year, a night at a Denver dance hall is a genuinely rare thing.

The timing is no accident. As Denver7 points out, the Grizzly Rose show lands the night before Ella's [Red Rocks date](/news/2026-09-23-ella-langley-red-rocks-morrison-set-time) in Morrison on October 7. She's effectively turning one Colorado stop into a two-night run: the club on Tuesday, the amphitheater on Wednesday.

## How to get in, and the deadline that matters

You don't buy a ticket to this one, you win it. SiriusXM is running a sweepstakes for pairs of tickets, and here's the date to circle: entries close Monday, September 29. That's the whole reason we're posting this at midday instead of sitting on it. If you're anywhere near Denver, go enter now. And if you're not close, SiriusXM says out-of-area fans can enter for a VIP trip to the show, so distance isn't a dealbreaker.

## Can't be there? You can still hear it

Even if you don't win, you're not shut out. SiriusXM will air Ella's full Grizzly Rose set on The Highway on Friday, October 9 at 8:00 PM ET, and it'll live on the SiriusXM app anytime after that. So the whole show, built on the same catalog that's kept [Choosin' Texas](/songs/choosin-texas) at the top of the charts all year, is coming to your speakers no matter what.

## Our take

This is the move of an artist who hasn't forgotten where she started, Alabama bar gigs to record-breaking arenas and now, on purpose, back into a room where you can practically see the whites of her eyes. For the rest of the fall's set times and tickets, our [tour page](/tour) has every remaining stop. But if you can swing Denver the first week of October, the Grizzly Rose is the ticket to chase.
