## Greek Theatre notes: parking, weather, and getting there

The Greek is an open-air amphitheatre of about 5,900 seats in Griffith Park, and two things are worth planning around. First, **parking** — the Greek uses stacked parking, which is famously slow to clear out, so rideshare or arriving early saves the headache. Second, **weather** — mid-October evenings in LA are usually mild but cool off after dark, so a light layer earns its keep by the encore.
