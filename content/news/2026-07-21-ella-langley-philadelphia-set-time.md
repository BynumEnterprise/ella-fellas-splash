**Saturday, August 1, 2026** — Ella Langley played Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia as direct support on Morgan Wallen's Still The Problem Tour. It was a stadium night, nearly 70,000 people, and as direct support she was on *before* Wallen — earlier than a lot of casual ticket-holders expected. That show has passed now; if you came here looking for her set time, here's how the night was built, plus where to see her next.

## Who played when

Four acts, and the listed start time was **5:30 PM** with doors at 4:30:

- **Blake Whiten** opened the night.
- **Hudson Westbrook** followed.
- **Ella Langley** was direct support — she played right before Wallen.
- **Morgan Wallen** closed.

The first act went on at or near that 5:30 listed start. With four acts and an early start, Ella — third on the bill — took the stage while it was still light out, not at 9 PM. Anyone who rolled in on "headliner time" caught her from the concourse rather than their seat.

For the record: the Linc hosted Wallen **two nights**, and Ella was only on the **Saturday, August 1** bill. Friday's show (July 31) had Brooks & Dunn in the support slot instead.

## What she's been playing

No fabricated setlist here. Support sets are tighter than her headline nights, and lately she's been packing them with the songs the stadium actually knows — "[you look like you love me](/songs/you-look-like-you-love-me)," "[Choosin' Texas](/songs/choosin-texas)," "[weren't for the wind](/songs/werent-for-the-wind)," "[Be Her](/songs/be-her)." She also joined Wallen during his set for "Sand in My Boots" at earlier stops that year.

## At the venue

The Linc is an open-air NFL stadium in the South Philly sports complex — August in Philadelphia means it'll be hot when Blake Whiten starts and sticky when Wallen ends, so hydrate before you're through the gates. The stadium runs an NFL-style clear-bag policy; check the venue's bag rules before you pack anything. Parking lots around the complex fill early on a sold-out stadium night, and the Broad Street Line drops you at NRG Station right there — for a 70,000-person show, the subway is the honest play. Seating map and sightlines are on the [set-times and where-to-sit page](/tour/philadelphia-lincoln-financial-field-2026-08-01/set-times).
