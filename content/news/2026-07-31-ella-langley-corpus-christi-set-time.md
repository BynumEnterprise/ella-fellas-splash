## Hilliard Center notes

Indoor arena, air conditioning, zero weather planning — in Corpus Christi in August, that matters. The building sits right on the water downtown, so the pre-show move is easy: get there early, walk the bayfront, then head in. A few house rules worth knowing before you're in line: clear bag policy, the building is fully cashless, and once you exit there's no re-entry.
