Short answer first, because it's the thing everyone types into Google: no, Ella Langley and Riley Green are not dating. They're friends, tourmates, and the two voices on one of the biggest country duets of the decade — and both of them have said, on the record, that the romance lives in the song and nowhere else. If you came here because "You Look Like You Love Me" made you wonder, you're in good company. That was kind of the point.

We're a fan site, not a rumor mill, so here's the honest version: what each of them has actually said, why the chemistry reads as real, and who Ella is really with as of late 2026.

## What Riley Green has said about dating Ella Langley

The clearest answer came straight from Riley. On *The Viall Files* podcast with Nick Viall and Natalie Joy, he was asked whether he and Ella are just friends, and his reply became the quote that's followed the pair ever since: "Yeah. I mean, Ella's probably too smart to date me anyway." (Holler, which covered the episode, published it in December 2024.)

He also said something more telling than the joke. According to that same interview, Riley told Ella early on that fans were always going to speculate, and that the two of them didn't need to feed it: "There are going to be a lot of things said, but I mean, we don't really gotta play into it." That's not the language of a couple protecting a secret. It's two artists who understood that a love song sung by two single people was going to light up the internet, and decided not to pretend otherwise in either direction.

## Why the chemistry feels so real

Here's the part that keeps the question alive: the chemistry isn't manufactured. It comes from the same place a lot of the best country duets do — a shared map. Both Riley and Ella grew up in Alabama, raised on the same radio, and when Riley has talked about why their voices lock together, he keeps coming back to that. In the story of how the song was made, he's blunt about it: he liked her voice, she was "obviously from Alabama. Very country," and the blend just worked.

The duet also wasn't the plan. Riley wrote "You Look Like You Love Me" and originally didn't intend for Ella to sing on it at all. As he told Apple Music, "I didn't plan on [Langley] being on that song" — he brought her into the studio to record a female vocal so he could hear the song that way and maybe pitch it elsewhere. Then she recorded it. "She just crushed it," he said, and the placeholder became the record. If you want the full breakdown of what the song is actually about, we dug into that in our [guide to the meaning of "You Look Like You Love Me"](/news/2026-09-13-ella-langley-you-look-like-you-love-me-meaning), and the track itself lives on the [Ella Langley song page](/songs/you-look-like-you-love-me).

Ella has been just as candid about the effect. She's said she and Riley figured the duet might stir up "some marriages, maybe some divorces" — a joke, but an honest read on what a song about lingering looks and barroom longing does to a crowd. When two people sell that convincingly, fans are going to assume it's true. That's a compliment to the performance, not evidence of a relationship.

## The awards, the second duet, and the touring

If the rumor won't die, it's partly because these two keep showing up together in ways that look like a couple's highlight reel. "You Look Like You Love Me" won Musical Event of the Year at the 2024 CMA Awards, and across that award season the song piled up a string of ACM and CMA honors — the kind of run that puts two names on the same stage, holding the same trophy, over and over.

Then came the second collaboration. On "Don't Mind If I Do," Riley again planned to swap Ella's vocal out for another singer, and again kept her: "I don't think anybody's gonna do it any better than that," he said. They performed it together on the finale of *The Voice* in December 2024 and have traded the two songs back and forth on tour. You can hear that second one on the [Ella Langley "Don't Mind If I Do" page](/songs/dont-mind-if-i-do). Two hit duets, a wall of awards, and a tour's worth of nightly performances will make any pairing look fated. It still doesn't make them a couple.

## So who is Ella Langley actually dating?

This is the detail that settles it. As of fall 2026, Ella isn't single — and the person she's with isn't Riley Green. Multiple outlets reported in September 2026 that Ella Langley and fellow country singer Tucker Wetmore are together, a confirmation that came from reporting rather than from either artist. We covered the whole thing in our breakdown of [Ella Langley and Tucker Wetmore dating](/news/2026-09-25-ella-langley-tucker-wetmore-dating), including the timeline and the Nashville night that set it off.

So the romance that fans keep projecting onto the Riley Green duet is, as it turns out, pointed at the wrong guy. Ella and Riley made a love song so convincing that people wrote a relationship into it. Ella's actual relationship is elsewhere, and Riley has spent two years gently telling anyone who'll listen that she's "too smart" to date him anyway.

## The bottom line

Ella Langley and Riley Green are creative partners and friends, not a couple — and both of them have said so in plain terms. The spark you hear on "You Look Like You Love Me" is real craft, built on a shared Alabama upbringing and a studio accident that turned into a CMA-winning hit. If you're new to all of this, the full story of how Ella got from Hope Hull bar gigs to the top of the charts is in our [Ella Langley biography](/about), and her current road schedule lives on the [Ella Langley tour page](/tour). The duet did its job a little too well. That's the only mystery here.
