It finally happened. "Choosin' Texas" has spent a 23rd week at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100, and with that week Ella Langley now owns the longest run at the top of the chart that anyone has ever had. Per [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/lists/ella-langley-choosin-texas-hot-100-number-one-chart-record/), the song passes Mariah Carey's "All I Want for Christmas Is You," which had sat alone at the top of the record book with 22 weeks. A country song about a girl choosing a state over a person is now the biggest No. 1 in the 67-year history of the Hot 100 — outright, holiday songs included.

## The record, in plain numbers

We have been [counting these weeks](/news/2026-09-12-ella-langley-choosin-texas-21-weeks-hot-100) for months, and every milestone we wrote up turned out to be a stop on the way to this one. It passed the longest run by a woman this century. It passed the all-time non-holiday mark of 16 weeks, shared until this summer by "One Sweet Day," "Despacito" and "Last Night." Two weeks ago it was one week short of Mariah's 22. Now it is one week past it.

The distinction that matters: Mariah's Christmas standard reached 22 weeks a few Decembers at a time, stacked across a decade of holiday seasons. "Choosin' Texas" has done 23 in a single unbroken run that started last winter and has not let go since. There is no longer a number in front of it.

## How a debut-era single got here

"Choosin' Texas" was released October 17, 2025 as the lead single from *Dandelion*, written by Langley with Luke Dick, Miranda Lambert and JoyBeth Taylor and produced by Lambert and Ben West. Nobody in that room was building a record-breaker; it was an album cut with a good hook and a strange true story behind it. If you're new to the song this fall, [what "Choosin' Texas" is actually about](/news/2026-07-22-choosin-texas-meaning) — kangaroo traffic stop and all — is the place to start, and the [full "Choosin' Texas" song page](/songs/choosin-texas) has every writer, credit and week of the run in one spot.

What has kept it up there is the part that still surprises us. Songs this deep into their chart life usually cool on streaming, slip on radio and drift down a spot at a time. This one refused to on all three fronts at once while she toured behind it every week.

## Langley's reaction

Langley, 27, has spent the whole run mostly stunned, and this week was no different. She told [WFMD](https://www.wfmd.com/2026/09/22/ella-langley-says-choosin-texas-success-is-changing-my-life-after-her-song-breaks-mariah-careys-record/) that the song's success is "changing my life," which is about the only honest thing you can say when your first big single passes Mariah Carey in the history books. There is no playbook for this — a country artist a couple of years into her career now holds a chart record that stood, untouched, since Mariah first led the Hot 100 with a Christmas song.
