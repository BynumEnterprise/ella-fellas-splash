We have been saying all year that 2026 belonged to Ella Langley, and the Recording Academy just made it official in the loudest way possible. When the 2027 Grammy nominations came out Monday morning, her name landed in all four of the general-field categories at once: Record of the Year, Album of the Year, Song of the Year, and Best New Artist. That is the full "Big Four," and almost nobody pulls it off.

How rare is it? Holler points out that she is the first country artist since Bobbie Gentry in 1968 to be nominated across all four of those marquee categories. Gentry did it with "Ode to Billie Joe" and walked away with Best New Artist. Now, 59 years later, a kid from Hope Hull, Alabama is in that same sentence.

## What she's nominated for

Her Record of the Year and Song of the Year nods both go to ["Choosin' Texas"](/songs/choosin-texas), the single that spent a record-setting run at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 this year. Album of the Year goes to [Dandelion](/songs/dandelion), the sophomore record that debuted at the top of the Billboard 200. And Best New Artist is the one we have been tracking for weeks — a summer rule change finally cleared her to compete, and the voters answered emphatically.

There is a nice footnote for Miranda Lambert here too. She co-produced Dandelion, co-wrote "Choosin' Texas," and picked up her own nomination for Producer of the Year, Non-Classical. The two of them are all over this ballot.

## When the ceremony happens

The 69th Grammy Awards go down Sunday, February 7, 2027, at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles — the first Grammy telecast on ABC (with a simulcast on Disney+ and Hulu) after more than five decades on CBS. Raye led the whole field with 11 nominations, per Variety, with Rosalia and Olivia Rodrigo close behind, so Ella is in deep, talented company.

Back on November 2 we broke down [her Best New Artist case](/news/2026-11-02-ella-langley-grammy-2027-best-new-artist) before the list came out — turns out the voters went even bigger. If you want to hear what got her here, start with "Choosin' Texas" and the rest of Dandelion, then check the [tour page](/tour) for where to catch her next.
