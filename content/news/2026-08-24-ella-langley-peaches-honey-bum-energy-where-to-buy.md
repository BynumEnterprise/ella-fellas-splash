If you have been trying to figure out where to actually buy Ella Langley's Peaches & Honey BUM Energy drink, here is the short version: it is on TikTok Shop right now, and it lands on shelves at major retailers nationwide on September 1. The Peaches & Honey flavor is her first energy-drink collaboration, a partnership with BUM Energy (the RAW Nutrition line), and she has been teasing it since those branded trailers started showing up outside Dandelion Tour stops earlier this year.

The name is not an accident. Peaches and honey both point straight back to her Alabama roots and Southern upbringing, which is the same well she keeps drawing from — the girl from [Hope Hull, Alabama](/news/2026-08-07-where-is-ella-langley-from-hope-hull-alabama) putting her home state on a can. Announcing it on her HQ Instagram, she wrote, "It's here!! Meet my new BUM Energy flavor, Peaches & Honey. Order exclusively online through the link in bio while supplies last or find it at retailers nationwide starting 9/1!"

## Where to buy it right now

Two ways, depending on how long you want to wait:

Online today — the drink is live on TikTok Shop, with cases starting around $23.00. That is the "order exclusively online" route she pointed fans to, and it is the only place to get it before September.

In stores September 1 — after that, it rolls out to retail nationwide. BUM Energy typically stocks in Walmart, Target, Costco and GNC, so those are the likeliest places to spot the Peaches & Honey can once the retail launch hits. If your local store carries the existing BUM flavors, that is where to check first.

## What's actually in the can

For anyone weighing it against the energy drink they already keep in the fridge, Peaches & Honey is a juicy peach flavor with a smooth, honey-inspired finish, and the specs are in line with the rest of the BUM line: zero sugar, 10 calories, and 125 milligrams of caffeine per can. That caffeine number sits in the same range as a strong cup of coffee, on the moderate side for the category.

RAW Nutrition CEO Domenic Iacovone said the flavor took months to dial in and admitted he had "major reservations" on it at first, before landing on something he now calls "absolutely insane" in the best way. Whether it delivers is a matter of taste, but the point of a collab like this is that it sounds like her, not like a committee.

## Why this fits where she is right now

This is not a random side quest. Langley is in the middle of a career year: ["Choosin' Texas" is still parked at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100](/news/2026-08-22-choosin-texas-18-weeks-number-one), and she is out on the fall leg of the [Dandelion Tour](/tour) playing to arenas night after night. A summery, hometown-flavored drink launching into that kind of visibility is about as good a runway as a debut flavor gets.
