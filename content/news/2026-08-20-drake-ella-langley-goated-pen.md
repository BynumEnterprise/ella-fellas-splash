Every so often two worlds we don't expect to overlap bump into each other, and this week it happened on Ella's Instagram. Drake — yes, that Drake — showed up in her comments and paid her about the highest compliment a songwriter can get.

Here's how it unfolded. On Saturday, Ella posted a TikTok of herself lip-syncing along to "HYFR (Hell Ya F***ing Right)," the Drake and Lil Wayne cut from his 2011 album Take Care. A day later, on Sunday, Drake turned up on one of her Instagram posts — a recap of her weekend Texas shows — and left a two-word comment that fans immediately screenshotted everywhere: "Goated pen," with a pen emoji.

If you're not fluent in internet, "goated pen" is a very big deal. "GOAT" means greatest of all time, and "pen" is shorthand for songwriting. So Drake, one of the most-streamed artists on the planet, essentially told Ella she's one of the best writers going. Her reply was perfectly on brand — a single shocked-face emoji, the same reaction most of us had. The comment piled up more than 4,000 likes fast, and the replies filled with one request over and over: put the two of them on a song together.

We've actually watched Drake circle Ella's music before. Back in June, [he was caught playing "Choosin' Texas" himself](/news/2026-06-25-drake-plays-choosin-texas), so this wasn't a random first encounter — he's clearly been paying attention to the run she's on. A few local country stations have already joked that he's officially an "Ella Fella" now, which, well, we'd like to think we called that first.

Will an actual collaboration happen? Nobody's confirmed a thing, and a rapper praising a country songwriter's pen doesn't automatically mean they're booking studio time. But the timing — her nodding to his catalog, him nodding right back within 24 hours — is the kind of mutual respect these things sometimes grow out of. For now it's a genuinely fun moment for a fanbase that has watched Ella's year get bigger by the week.
