The Texas run opens **Thursday, August 13, 2026** at Moody Center in Austin — indoors, air-conditioned, and roughly fifteen thousand people deep. After a summer of amphitheatre lawns and stadium support slots, this is Ella headlining a real arena in the state she wrote a song about. Here's the timing and the plan.

## Who plays when

Moody Center has posted one number for this night, and it's the one that matters most:

- **7:00 PM CT** — show starts (the venue's own posted time)
- Doors generally open about an hour ahead of the posted start

The bill, per the venue's event page, is **Gabriella Rose** first, then **Kaitlin Butts** in the direct-support slot, then **Ella Langley**.

What we're *not* going to do is invent a minute for when Ella walks out. Moody Center has published the show time and not a per-act running order, so 7:00 PM is the honest, sourced answer — the first opener goes on at the time printed on your ticket. On recent three-act Dandelion nights Ella has closed after both openers, which puts her comfortably into the back half of the evening, but a guessed stage-time minute is worth nothing to you and we'd rather say so. If the venue posts a full schedule closer to the date, this page and the [set-times page](/tour/austin-moody-center-2026-08-13/set-times) get the real times.

Worth showing up early regardless. Kaitlin Butts is not a warm-up act you skip — she's got her own following and a set that earns the room, and Gabriella Rose is exactly the kind of opener you'll be glad you caught before everyone else does.

## Moody Center notes

This is the easy one on the schedule: indoor arena, climate controlled, no weather to plan around. It sits on the UT campus at 2001 Robert Dedman Dr, which means campus-area traffic and parking that's worth sorting out before you leave the house — Moody runs its own parking pre-sale, and the surrounding garages fill up. There's a clear-bag policy, so check it rather than discovering it in line.
