**Short answer:** Ella Langley has **three confirmed 2027 dates** so far, all festivals. She headlines **Sunday, April 18 at Two Step Inn** in Georgetown, Texas. She's one of the three headliners at **Sand In My Boots, May 21-23 in Gulf Shores, Alabama** (her day hasn't been announced). And she plays the **Patriotic Festival in Virginia Beach on Saturday, May 29**. She's said she'll tour in 2027, but there's no headline tour announced yet. *Updated November 3, 2026.*

## Confirmed Ella Langley 2027 dates

- **Sun, April 18, 2027 - Georgetown, TX** - Two Step Inn (Sunday headliner). [Show page and tickets](/tour/georgetown-two-step-inn-festival-2027-04-18)
- **May 21-23, 2027 - Gulf Shores, AL** - Sand In My Boots (co-headliner; day TBA). [Show page and trip planning](/tour/gulf-shores-sand-in-my-boots-2027-05-21)
- **Sat, May 29, 2027 - Virginia Beach, VA** - Patriotic Festival. [Show page and tickets](/tour/virginia-beach-patriotic-festival-2027-05-29)

That's every 2027 date announced so far. We add dates the day they're officially announced, and we don't list rumors. The Sand In My Boots page is listed under May 21, the first day of the festival, until the festival says which day she plays.

## Sand In My Boots 2027 (Gulf Shores, AL)

Morgan Wallen's beach festival is back after a year off. The 2027 lineup came out November 2, with **Wallen, Kings of Leon and Ella Langley** as the three headliners of a 33-act bill. Kygo, Nelly, Koe Wetzel, Cole Swindell, Third Eye Blind and her Dandelion Tour opener Kameron Marlowe are also on it. Ella played the first Sand In My Boots in 2025. It's on the public beach at 101 E Beach Blvd, and there's no on-site parking, so plan to stay close or take the shuttle.

**Passes go on sale Friday, November 6 at 10 a.m. CT.** You have to register at sandinmybootsfest.com first. There's also a local presale on November 5 for eligible Gulf Coast ZIP codes. These are weekend passes, with all-in prices that include taxes and fees: GA is $676.87, and the tiers go up through Party Pit, VIP and Super VIP to $7,698.90 for Livin' the Dream. Payment plans are available. [Our full Sand In My Boots guide](/news/2026-11-03-ella-langley-sand-in-my-boots-2027-lineup-passes) has every tier, the age rules and where to stay.

## Two Step Inn 2027 (Georgetown, TX)

Two Step Inn returns to San Gabriel Park in Georgetown on April 17-18, 2027. Brooks & Dunn top Saturday, and **Ella Langley closes the festival on Sunday, April 18**. The Sunday lineup announced so far also includes Sheryl Crow, Flatland Cavalry, Clint Black, Michelle Branch, Jo Dee Messina and Old Crow Medicine Show. Gates open at 11 a.m. each day per the ticket listing. Set times and stage assignments haven't been released. You can buy a single-day Sunday pass or a 2-day pass.

## Patriotic Festival 2027 (Virginia Beach, VA)

The Patriotic Festival returns to the Virginia Beach Oceanfront in 2027 with Ella Langley, Riley Green and Bailey Zimmerman headlining. Ella's night is **Saturday, May 29, 2027** (Memorial Day weekend), which is about a week after Sand In My Boots. Set times haven't been announced.

## Is Ella Langley touring in 2027?

Yes, according to her. In a livestream in early October, a fan asked whether she'd tour next year. "Am I going on tour next year? Have I gone on tour every year? Of course! Who would I be? Who would I be if I didn't go to sleep at terrible hours and go to sleep in a different state every night?" she said, as [Country Now](https://countrynow.com/ella-langley-has-no-plans-to-slow-down-confirms-shell-tour-again-in-2027/) reported on October 9 and [Taste of Country](https://tasteofcountry.com/ella-langley-2027-tour-plans/) followed up.

What she hasn't done is announce dates. Beyond the three festivals above, there's no 2027 headline tour, no cities and no presale. Any site with a full 2027 Ella Langley itinerary right now is guessing.

If 2026 is any guide, a tour will be announced in stages, with a fan presale through her free official mailing list before each public on-sale. [Here's how her presales work](/guides/ella-langley-fan-club).
