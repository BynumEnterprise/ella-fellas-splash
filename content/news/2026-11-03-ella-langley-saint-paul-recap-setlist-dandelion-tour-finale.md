The Dandelion Tour ended on Halloween night. Ella Langley closed it out on Saturday, October 31 at **Grand Casino Arena in Saint Paul**, dressed as **Jessie from *Toy Story***, with her band in costume as the rest of the toys. She played a **19-song main set and a two-song encore**, and Gabriella Rose and Kameron Marlowe both came out for duets. It was her last show of 2026.

## Ella Langley's Toy Story Halloween costume

Her band and crew voted on a group costume, and *Toy Story* won, [Taste of Country reports](https://tasteofcountry.com/see-ella-langleys-incredible-toy-story-costume-at-her-halloween-show-watch/). Ella was Jessie, with the cow print, the Western shirt and the wide red hat. That's not much of a stretch for her. Her bandmates were Woody, Buzz Lightyear and the others. Kameron Marlowe came as Mario from Super Mario Bros. and sang "Strangers" with her in costume. Her parents dressed up too, and according to fan video, they came onstage at one point to do a shot.

## "I ain't trying to trip and die"

Fans kept throwing Halloween candy onto the stage, and she eventually made them stop. "Where does all this candy keep coming from?" she asked in a fan-filmed TikTok clip, [via Taste of Country](https://tasteofcountry.com/ella-langley-halloween-candy-thrown-onstage/). Then she picked a piece up and tossed it back toward the front row: "I know it's Halloween, but I ain't trying to trip and die."

So please don't throw candy at her.

## How the Dandelion Tour ended

Saint Paul was the final date of a year spent mostly on the road. She played spring festivals, opened stadium shows for Morgan Wallen, and headlined the Dandelion Tour from May through October. The last week was Des Moines on October 29, then Lincoln on October 30. [Lincoln's setlist](/news/2026-10-31-ella-langley-lincoln-recap-setlist) still hasn't been logged on setlist.fm. Earlier in October, [Tulsa was canceled](/news/2026-10-09-ella-langley-tulsa-concert-canceled-security-threat) hours before showtime over a security threat. That's the one big gap in the run.
