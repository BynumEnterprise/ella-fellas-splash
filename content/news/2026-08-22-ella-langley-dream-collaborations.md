Every time Ella Langley sits down for an interview lately, someone asks the fun question: who's left on the wish list? In a new conversation with *Variety*, she finally answered it, and the names she gave say a lot about where her head is at right now. Ask about Ella Langley's dream collaborations and you don't get a safe list of country radio staples — you get Stevie Nicks, Chris Stapleton and Harry Styles, three artists who live in three different corners of music.

That spread is the whole point. Her own records already slide between honky-tonk, soul, and something looser and more pop-leaning, so it tracks that the people she's chasing don't sit in one lane either.

## The Three Names on Her List

Stevie Nicks is the swing-for-the-fences pick. The biggest female voice in rock and roll teaming up with one of the biggest new voices in country would be a genuine event — and given how "Silver Springs" keeps finding new life with younger listeners, the two audiences overlap more than you'd think.

Chris Stapleton feels like the most likely to actually happen. He guests generously, his voice bends toward soul the same way Ella's does on her slower material, and you can hear the blend before either of them cuts a note. Of the three, this is the one fans should probably keep an eye on.

Harry Styles is the wild card. There's no obvious template for what a Langley–Styles song sounds like, which is exactly why it's interesting. And there's a neat bit of chart symmetry here: Ella's long run at No. 1 with "Choosin' Texas" put her name right next to Styles' in the Hot 100 record books, as [Rolling Stone laid out](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-country/ella-langley-choosin-texas-chart-history-harry-styles-1235599161/). The admiration isn't coming out of nowhere.

## She's Already a Serious Duet Partner

The dream list lands differently when you remember how good Ella already is at sharing a song. Her breakout, ["You Look Like You Love Me"](/songs/you-look-like-you-love-me) with Riley Green, is what put her on most people's radar in the first place. Her duet with Morgan Wallen, ["I Can't Love You Anymore,"](/songs/i-cant-love-you-anymore) climbed to No. 2 on Country Airplay this summer. And she just reunited with Koe Wetzel for ["Jaded,"](/news/2026-08-05-koe-wetzel-ella-langley-jaded-duet) their funkiest pairing yet.

Then there's Miranda Lambert, who's been less a one-off feature than a genuine mentor across this whole run — we [dug into that relationship here](/news/2026-08-21-ella-langley-miranda-lambert-mentorship). Point being: when Ella talks about wanting to work with Stevie Nicks or Chris Stapleton, she's not a newcomer hoping for a co-sign. She's already proven she can hold her own next to just about anyone.

## Would Any of These Actually Happen?

Stapleton is the realistic bet, Nicks is the dream, and Styles is the one nobody can quite picture — which is why fans keep bringing it up. None are confirmed, and Ella framed them as hopes rather than plans, so treat any specifics you see floating around online with a grain of salt until there's an actual announcement.
