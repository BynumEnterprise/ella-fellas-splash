Ella Langley played two nights at the **Greek Theatre in Los Angeles**, Tuesday, October 13 and Wednesday, October 14, with Laci Kaye Booth and Kameron Marlowe opening. Night one was **sold out**. The *Los Angeles Times* reviewed it, and the review was mixed: the critic loved the acoustic section in the middle of the set and was much less impressed by the rest. **Neither night has a setlist on setlist.fm yet.** We're not going to guess the order.

## Night one: the acoustic set

About halfway through the Tuesday show, Langley pulled a stool to the front of the stage and told the crowd she wanted to "be kind of normal for a second, if that's all right witcha," [LA Times pop critic Mikael Wood wrote](https://dnyuz.com/2026/10/14/ella-langley-ponders-the-dream-at-l-a-s-greek-theatre/). She played "Somethin' Simple" from *Dandelion* ("While I'm living the dream, all I'm dreaming about is slowing down just a little"). Then she brought out both openers for stripped-down duets: Fleetwood Mac's "Dreams" with Laci Kaye Booth and "Strangers" with Kameron Marlowe.

Wood called that stretch "by far the best part of the night." He also said Marlowe "gladly disregarded that unspoken rule about not showing up the headliner who hired you."

## The rest of the review

He didn't like the rest as much. Wood wrote that the bigger, fully produced parts of the show "demanded a level of star power that Langley simply doesn't possess (or anyway isn't interested in deploying)." He also said her cool, detached delivery, which works on record, came across as flat onstage. He did like how she looked ("very Linda Ronstadt meets Stevie Nicks"). He also noticed how much merch there was: more merch stands than he'd ever seen at the Greek, and "every one of them was jammed."

That's one critic on one night, and he still described a packed, sold-out room. It's a real review, though, and you should know what it says.

## A young fan's "Butterfly Season" moment

The clip from LA that went viral wasn't from the review. At one of the Greek shows, Langley invited a young fan named Mallory onstage to help her sing ["Butterfly Season,"](/songs/butterfly-season) [Entertainment Now reported](https://entertainmentnow.com/country-music/ella-langley-dandelion-tour-los-angeles-young-fan/) from a TikTok video. Mallory wore pink cowboy boots and an Ella T-shirt, and she sang the "I'm finding my wings" line back to her. "Y'all give it up for Mallory tonight!" Langley told the crowd. Then: "That's the most precious angel I've ever seen." The report doesn't say which of the two nights it was.
