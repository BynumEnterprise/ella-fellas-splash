The Dandelion Tour rolled into Oxford on Thursday night, and Ella Langley played The Sandy and John Black Pavilion at Ole Miss with Kameron Marlowe and Laci Kaye Booth on the bill — an Alabama girl a couple hours down the road from home, playing to a Mississippi college crowd on a school night. That combination usually means loud, and Oxford tends to bring it.

We're publishing this the morning after, and a full song-by-song capture from the floor hadn't hit setlist.fm yet by the time we hit go. So here's the honest version: this is the setlist Ella has been running on The Dandelion Tour all fall, the shape you should expect from the Pavilion show, with the guest spots her Oxford lineup sets up. We'll update the order if a verified Oxford list posts.

## The Dandelion Tour setlist (what Ella's been playing in 2026)

She opens on a taped intro — BigXthaPlug's "Hell at Night" bleeding into the old traditional "Froggy Went a Courtin'" — before the band kicks in live on the title track. Per Holler's tour setlist (captured at Canandaigua, July 21), the run has been:

1. Dandelion
2. I Gotta Quit
3. Bottom of Your Boots
4. Here for the Party (Gretchen Wilson cover)
5. Country Boy's Dream Girl
6. girl you're taking home
7. 20-20
8. You & Me Time
9. Broken
10. Last Call for Us
11. Strangers (with Kameron Marlowe)
12. Butterfly Season
13. Dreams (Fleetwood Mac cover, with Laci Kaye Booth)
14. Be Her
15. you look like you love me
16. Loving Life Again
17. Choosin' Texas
18. weren't for the wind

Encore:

19. love you tonight
20. That's Why We Fight

(Standard tour order per Holler; the exact Oxford sequence and any audibles weren't confirmed at publish time.)

## The moments to watch for

Two guest spots are baked into this lineup. Kameron Marlowe has been coming back out for "Strangers," the duet the two of them cut together, and Laci Kaye Booth joins Ella for a cover of Fleetwood Mac's "Dreams" — the kind of two-women-and-a-Rumours-song moment that gets a room quiet in the best way. With both of them on the Oxford bill, those are the pair we'd have had our eyes on.

The back half is where she stacks the big ones. "Be Her" into "you look like you love me" — the Riley Green duet that put her on the map — into "Loving Life Again" and "Choosin' Texas" is about as strong a four-song close as she's got, and "Choosin' Texas" has been living near the top of the Hot 100 basically all year, so the Pavilion floor almost certainly sang every word back at her. She's been saving "love you tonight" and the Koe Wetzel collab "That's Why We Fight" for the encore.
