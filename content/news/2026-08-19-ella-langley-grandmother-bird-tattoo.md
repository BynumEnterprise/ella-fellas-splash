Ella's arms already read like a scrapbook, so a new tattoo isn't unusual. This one, though, comes with a story worth telling. Ahead of her sold-out show at Austin's Moody Center on August 13, she sat down backstage and added a small bird perched on a branch to her forearm, a tribute to the grandmother who set her on the path to music in the first place.

## Where the bird comes from

The design isn't random. According to tattoo artist Hannah Matthews, who did the piece, the little bird is based on a picture Ella's grandmother kept. If you've followed Ella's family story, the choice lands even harder. Her paternal grandparents were both musical: her grandmother sang, and her grandfather could pick up just about any instrument and play it by ear. After her grandfather passed, his guitar was restrung and handed down to Ella. The first song she ever taught herself on it was Bob Marley's "Three Little Birds," which makes a small bird on her arm feel less like a coincidence and more like a full circle.

## You've already heard her grandma

Here's the detail that gets us. That grandmother isn't just tucked away in family memory, she's on the record. On "Loving Life Again," the third single off Dandelion, Ella folded in the familiar "yoo-hoo" her grandma used to call out whenever the family went hunting for her around the house. Once you know it's there, you can't unhear it. It's the kind of touch that explains why this album has connected the way it has, and why "Loving Life Again" keeps climbing radio right now.

For a run of 2026 that has been all charts, [VMA nominations](/news/2026-08-19-ella-langley-vma-nominations-choosin-texas), and sold-out arenas, this is a quieter, warmer reminder of where the songs actually come from. Ella has been open all year about carrying her family's fingerprints into the music, and now she's carrying one of them on her skin too.

If you're catching a show on the road this fall, the [Dandelion Tour](/tour) picks back up August 20 in Green Bay. And if you've ever wondered how a girl from a small Alabama town ended up here, the answer keeps tracing back to family, [all the way to Hope Hull](/news/2026-08-07-where-is-ella-langley-from-hope-hull-alabama).
