The New England leg lands **Thursday, July 30, 2026** at BankNH Pavilion in Gilford, NH — a covered-pavilion-plus-lawn amphitheatre on Meadowbrook. Here's the timing and the outdoor-show plan.

## Who plays when

The venue has now published the night's full running order — these are BankNH Pavilion's own posted times, not our guesses:

- **4:30 PM** — parking opens
- **5:00 PM** — doors open
- **5:00 PM** — **Whitney Doucet** on the Hazy Little Stage (the pre-show side stage, out on the plaza)
- **7:10 PM** — **Laci Kaye Booth**
- **7:50 PM** — **Kameron Marlowe**
- **9:00 PM** — **Ella Langley**

So if you only care about one number: **Ella goes on at 9:00 PM.** Everything before that is a bonus, and Kameron Marlowe in the direct-support slot is a real draw in his own right — this is a get-there-early bill, not a show-up-at-nine one.

Two things worth knowing. Venue-posted times can still slide by a few minutes on the night, so treat 9:00 as the target and not a stopwatch. And entry runs in order — Artist VIP, Venue VIP, Fast Lane, then general population — starting right at doors, so if you're in a VIP package check your producer's documentation, since VIP entry sometimes opens before the posted 5:00.

## Outdoor-show tips for BankNH Pavilion

BankNH is a lakes-region amphitheatre with a covered pavilion and a lawn, so weather is part of the plan — a late-July New Hampshire evening can cool off after dark, and lawn seats mean bringing whatever the venue allows. Check the clear-bag and lawn-chair policy before you leave. For seating and where the sightlines are best, see the [set-times and where-to-sit page](/tour/gilford-banknh-pavilion-2026-07-30/set-times).
