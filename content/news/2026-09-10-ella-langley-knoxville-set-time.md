## Food City Center notes

It's a large indoor arena on the UT campus, climate controlled, so there's no weather to worry about. Parking around campus fills up fast for a full house — give yourself 45 minutes to an hour before the 7:00 start, follow the event-parking signage, and screenshot your mobile ticket before you're in a dead-signal lot. The room runs cool once it's full and air-conditioned; a light layer helps.
