## Greek Theatre notes: parking, weather, and getting there

The Greek is an open-air amphitheatre of around 5,900 seats in Griffith Park. Plan around two things: **parking**, which is stacked and slow to clear — rideshare or an early arrival saves the headache — and **weather**, since mid-October LA evenings are mild but cool off after dark, so bring a light layer for the back half of the night.
