Ella Langley brings The Dandelion Tour to **Neville Arena in Auburn on Friday, August 28, 2026** — the first of two nights on The Plains, and about as close to a hometown show as this tour gets. Ella spent two years at Auburn studying forestry before she left for Nashville, so this one lands a little differently for her and for everyone in the building. Here's the timing and the plan.

## Who plays when

Auburn Athletics and AXS list the show start, and that's the number to build your night around:

- **7:00 PM CT** — show starts (the ticketed start time)
- **Doors** open roughly an hour before, so plan to be inside around 6:00 PM

The bill, per the venue's announcement, is **Gabriella Rose** first, then **ERNEST** in the direct-support slot, then **Ella Langley**.

We're not going to invent a minute for when Ella walks out — Neville Arena hasn't posted a per-act running order, only the 7:00 PM start. That's the honest, sourced answer: the opener goes on at the printed start time, and on recent three-act Dandelion nights Ella has closed the evening after both openers. If the venue posts a full schedule closer to the date, this page and the [set-times page](/tour/auburn-neville-arena-2026-08-28/set-times) will get the real times.

Come early for the openers. **ERNEST** is one of Nashville's most-cut songwriters with a hit catalog of his own, and putting him in the direct-support slot means the room will already be loud by the time Ella takes over. **Gabriella Rose** opens the night and is worth catching from the first song.

## Neville Arena notes

Easy one to plan for: indoor arena, climate controlled, no weather to worry about. Neville Arena sits at 250 Beard-Eaves Court on Auburn's campus — expect the usual college-town traffic around a big event, so give yourself extra time to park and walk in. Travel light on bags; arena clear-bag policies move you through security faster.
