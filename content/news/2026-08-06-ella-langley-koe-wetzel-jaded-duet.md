The pairing that gave us "That's Why We Fight" is back. Ella Langley and Koe Wetzel are reuniting on a new duet called "Jaded," and it lands Friday, August 7, as part of the deluxe edition of Wetzel's album *The Night Champion*, [per Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/country/ella-langley-koe-wetzel-new-song-jaded-release-date-1236309579/). For anyone who has been waiting years for these two voices to share a track again, the wait is nearly over.

## What We Know About "Jaded"

"Jaded" was written by Wetzel, Langley, Jordan Schmidt and ERNEST, [according to reporting on the release](https://americansongwriter.com/koe-wetzel-announces-highly-anticipated-new-song-jaded-with-chart-topper-ella-langley/). It arrives on the expanded version of *The Night Champion*, so the duet is the headline addition to a record fans already have on repeat. Wetzel teased the collaboration earlier in the week, and [Whiskey Riff confirmed](https://www.whiskeyriff.com/2026/08/05/koe-wetzel-reunites-with-ella-langley-for-new-duet-jaded-dropping-this-friday/) the Friday release the day the news broke.

The title alone tells you the lane. Wetzel built his name on ragged, honest heartache, and Langley has spent the past year proving she can match anyone in the room for raw delivery, whether she is trading lines with a duet partner or singing a solo cut like ["Choosin' Texas."](/songs/choosin-texas) A song called "Jaded" from these two is not going to be a polite little love song.

## Why This Reunion Matters

This is the second time Langley and Wetzel have shared a song. Their first, "That's Why We Fight," appeared on Langley's 2023 debut EP *Excuse the Mess* and helped introduce a lot of listeners to her before the arenas and the chart records. Circling back now, with Langley in the middle of a headlining tour and a run of radio hits, turns a scrappy early collaboration into a full-circle moment.

Wetzel is not shy about the chemistry either. [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-features/koe-wetzel-favorite-duet-partner-ella-langley-interview-1235603905/) framed the reunion around him calling Langley his favorite duet partner, which is a fair thing to say about the person you keep coming back to sing with. The two of them clearly hear something in each other's voices that works, and "Jaded" is the third chapter after "That's Why We Fight" and years of mutual shout-outs.

## Where "Jaded" Fits in Langley's 2026

Langley has spent 2026 stacking duets and radio milestones on top of a packed touring calendar. Her collaboration with Morgan Wallen, ["I Can't Love You Anymore,"](/songs/i-cant-love-you-anymore) has been climbing country radio all summer, and she is closing out the first leg of the Dandelion Tour with a Texas run that wraps in Fort Worth. Dropping a new Koe Wetzel duet in the same stretch keeps her name on new-release lists without slowing the tour down.
