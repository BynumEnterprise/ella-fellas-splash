We said last week that the chart dated August 29 was the one to watch, and it did not disappoint. On that Hot 100, ["Choosin' Texas"](/songs/choosin-texas) holds at No. 1 for a 19th week — which means Ella Langley has now tied the longest No. 1 run in Hot 100 history for a non-holiday song, [as Billboard confirmed](https://www.billboard.com/lists/ella-langley-choosin-texas-hot-100-number-one-19-weeks/). A country single that started climbing back in February is now level with the biggest crossover smashes of the streaming era.

The 19-week mark had been shared by Lil Nas X's "Old Town Road" and Shaboozey's "A Bar Song (Tipsy)" — two songs that felt, at the time, like once-in-a-decade cultural events. "Choosin' Texas" has now joined them at the very top of that list. The only reign that still sits above hers over a full calendar is Mariah Carey's "All I Want for Christmas Is You," and that one banked its 22 weeks across multiple holiday seasons. What Langley has done, she's done in one unbroken stretch, in a single year.

## What Tying This Record Actually Means

A tie at 19 weeks is not a technicality — it puts "Choosin' Texas" in a club of exactly three non-holiday songs in the entire history of the Hot 100. When the song passed Mariah Carey and Boyz II Men's "One Sweet Day" at 17 weeks earlier this month, Variety noted it had climbed [into fourth place all-time](https://variety.com/2026/music/news/ella-langley-choosin-texas-17th-week-hot-100-1236831734/). Two weeks later she isn't in fourth anymore — she's tied for first among everything that isn't a Christmas perennial. We walked through the 18th-week stakes [in last week's write-up](/news/2026-08-22-choosin-texas-18-weeks-number-one); this is the week the "one away" turned into "even."

## Why It Won't Let Go

The Hot 100 blends streaming, radio airplay and sales, and "Choosin' Texas" keeps turning up strong across all three rather than leaning on any single format. That balance is the whole reason a song holds this long instead of spiking and fading. It's also why her catalog keeps rising in its wake — we covered the week [country swept the entire Hot 100 top five](/news/2026-08-08-ella-langley-country-hot-100-top-5-sweep), and the engine behind that sweep is the same single still parked at the summit.
