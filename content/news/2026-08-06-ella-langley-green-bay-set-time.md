The Resch Center has posted the number that matters, and it's the one to build your night around:

## Resch Center notes

This is an easy one to plan for: indoor arena, climate controlled, no weather to worry about. It sits in the Resch Complex right across from Lambeau Field, so parking is straightforward but cashless — figure **$15 in the Lambeau Field lot or $25 in the NV Technologies lot** across from the arena. Bags are limited to **8.5" x 11"** and smaller moves through security faster, so travel light.

Two things specific to this show worth knowing before you buy: tickets have **delayed mobile delivery** (the venue sends them out roughly two weeks ahead, so don't panic if they're not in your wallet right away), and there's a **six-ticket-per-household limit**.

For where to sit — lower bowl versus the upper level, and which sections get the best sound — see the [Green Bay set-times and where-to-sit page](/tour/green-bay-resch-center-2026-08-20/set-times).
