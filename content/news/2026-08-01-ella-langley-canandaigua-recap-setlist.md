You know a show is big when the sheriff's office gets involved. The Ontario County Sheriff put out a traffic alert ahead of Friday night, warning everyone near Canandaigua to expect delays around CMAC — because Ella Langley sold the place out. Lots opened at 3:30, gates at 5:30, and by the 7:00 start the lawn at the Finger Lakes' biggest amphitheater was full.

That's the shape of this summer in one night. A year ago she was playing these markets as an opener. Friday she had her name alone on the marquee at a venue that holds 15,000, and it wasn't enough seats.

## How the night ran

CMAC had the bill posted straight from the source: Laci Kaye Booth and Kameron Marlowe as special guests, doors at 5:30, music at 7:00, with Ella closing the night. It's the same undercard that ran the Gilford show on Thursday, and it's a strong one — Marlowe's been pulling his own headline crowds all year, and Booth's set has been quietly winning over early arrivals the whole tour.

Back-to-back sold-out amphitheater nights in New Hampshire and upstate New York, with Philadelphia's Lincoln Financial Field the very next day. That's the stretch she's in right now.

## The setlist

The fan-logged Canandaigua setlist hasn't hit setlist.fm yet — we'll update this page the moment it does. But her Dandelion Tour set has barely moved all summer, so here's what she played at her most recent logged headline show (Wilmington, NC, June 26, per setlist.fm), and Friday almost certainly ran close to it:

1. Dandelion
2. I Gotta Quit
3. Bottom of Your Boots
4. Country Boy's Dream Girl
5. girl you're taking home
6. 20-20
7. You & Me Time
8. Last Call for Us
9. Here for the Party (Gretchen Wilson cover)
10. Broken
11. Somethin' Simple
12. Butterfly Season
13. Speaking Terms (acoustic)
14. Be Her
15. you look like you love me
16. Loving Life Again
17. Choosin' Texas
18. [weren't for the wind](/songs/werent-for-the-wind)

That closing run — "you look like you love me" through "weren't for the wind" — has ended nearly every show on this tour, and per setlist.fm's 2026 stats, "weren't for the wind" has closed 23 of her last 26 logged sets. If Friday broke that streak, we want to hear about it.
