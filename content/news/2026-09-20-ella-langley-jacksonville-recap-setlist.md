Third night in three days, and Ella still had plenty in the tank. The Dandelion Tour wrapped its Southeast swing at VyStar Veterans Memorial Arena in Jacksonville on Saturday, one night after Athens and two after Oxford, with Kameron Marlowe and Laci Kaye Booth rounding out the bill. We've got this one verified song-by-song from a setlist.fm capture off the floor, so what's below is the real Jacksonville order — and this time both openers came back out during Ella's set.

She opened the same way she has all fall, straight into "Dandelion" and then "I Gotta Quit" and "Bottom of Your Boots," with the Morgan Wallen collab "I Can't Love You Anymore" landing at the four-spot again. The middle of the set is where Jacksonville got its own night: back-to-back duets, one with each opener.

## The moments

Two guest spots back to back, and both were the real deal. Kameron Marlowe came out for "Strangers," his own song, at number 12 — that's been the reliable duet on this run. Then Laci Kaye Booth stuck around for a cover of Fleetwood Mac's "Dreams" right after, which is a gorgeous pairing of voices and not something every crowd on this tour has gotten. Getting both openers on stage inside a three-song stretch is about as generous as an opener slot gets.

Four covers in all: Gretchen Wilson's "Here for the Party" mid-set, "Strangers" and "Dreams" back to back, and Toby Keith's "Wish I Didn't Know Now" to kick off the encore. That Toby Keith cut has become her go-to encore opener, a nice bit of old-school country before she brings it home.

The closing run is the reason you buy the ticket. "Be Her" into "you look like you love me" — the Riley Green duet that broke her wide open — into "Loving Life Again" and then "Choosin' Texas" is a murderer's row, and "Choosin' Texas" has spent most of 2026 near the top of the Hot 100, so the VyStar floor almost certainly sang the whole thing back at her. She ended the main set on "weren't for the wind" and closed the night on "That's Why We Fight," the Koe Wetzel collab that's been her gut-punch finale all fall.
