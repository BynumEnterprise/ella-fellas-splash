# What Ella Langley Played at North Charleston Coliseum

The North Charleston setlist took a couple of days to land on setlist.fm, so here it is in full: Ella Langley played 19 songs plus a two-song encore at North Charleston Coliseum on Saturday, July 25, with Kaitlin Butts and Laci Kaye Booth on the bill, per [setlist.fm](https://www.setlist.fm/setlist/ella-langley/2026/north-charleston-coliseum-north-charleston-sc-2b4bf866.html).

If you're headed to Gilford, Canandaigua or Philadelphia this week, skip to the bottom — the comparison with the night before is the useful part.

1. Dandelion
2. I Gotta Quit
3. Bottom of Your Boots
4. Here for the Party *(Gretchen Wilson cover)*
5. girl you're taking home
6. 20-20
7. You & Me Time

9. Country Boy's Dream Girl
10. That's Why We Fight
11. Broken
12. Somethin' Simple *(acoustic)*
13. Butterfly Season *(acoustic, with Kaitlin Butts)*
14. Dreams *(Fleetwood Mac cover, acoustic, with Laci Kaye Booth)*
15. Be Her
16. you look like you love me
17. Loving Life Again
18. Choosin' Texas
19. weren't for the wind

**Encore:** love you tonight, then paint the town blue.

## The acoustic middle is the best-kept secret of this tour

Songs 12 through 14 are the part nobody warns you about. She pulls the volume down for "Somethin' Simple," brings [Kaitlin Butts](https://www.setlist.fm/setlist/ella-langley/2026/north-charleston-coliseum-north-charleston-sc-2b4bf866.html) out for "Butterfly Season," and then she and Laci Kaye Booth do Fleetwood Mac's "Dreams" — three acoustic songs in a row in the middle of an arena show.

We'd flag this for anyone planning a beer run or a bathroom break. Do it during "Last Call for Us." Do not do it at song 12.

## Note where "Choosin' Texas" sits

It is song 18, not the closer. "weren't for the wind" ends the main set, and the encore is "love you tonight" into "paint the town blue."

That is a real choice on her part. The song that just [broke Whitney Houston and Mariah Carey's Hot 100 record with a 15th week at No. 1](/news/2026-07-28-ella-langley-choosin-texas-15-weeks-record) is not being used as the mic-drop. It goes near the end of the main set, and then she plays a quieter song after it and comes back with two more. If you want to know what the [song itself is about](/songs/choosin-texas), that's a separate rabbit hole.

## What this means if your show is this week

Here's the thing we can actually tell you that the setlist sites don't: **the set is locked in.** Compare North Charleston to [Cary the night before](https://www.setlist.fm/setlist/ella-langley/2026/koka-booth-amphitheatre-cary-nc-43743b37.html) and it's the same 19 songs and the same two-song encore. The only change is one swap in the middle — "That's Why We Fight" and "Country Boy's Dream Girl" moved from slots 5 and 6 in Cary down to 9 and 10 in North Charleston.

Two shows, back to back, essentially identical. So if you're going to [Gilford on Thursday](/tour/gilford-banknh-pavilion-2026-07-30), Canandaigua on Friday or Philadelphia on Saturday, this list is a reliable preview of your night. Learn the words to "weren't for the wind" — it's the one everybody's still singing on the way out.

One caveat on timing: setlist.fm logged a 9:15 PM start and an 11:00 PM finish for the Cary show specifically. We haven't seen confirmed start times for the other dates, so check your venue rather than assuming that clock travels.
