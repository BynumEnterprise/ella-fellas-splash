Ella Langley closes The Dandelion Tour in Saint Paul tonight — Saturday, October 31 — at Grand Casino Arena, with Gabriella Rose and Kameron Marlowe along for the send-off. It's the final date of the run and Ella's first Minnesota headline show, landing on Halloween, so expect a big night. Here's the plan for the Saint Paul set time: when the music starts, who's on when, where to sit, and where tickets stand. For the whole run, see [The Dandelion Tour schedule](/tour).

## Saint Paul set time: who plays when

Grand Casino Arena has the night set for **doors at 6:00 PM and show at 7:00 PM** — those are the venue's own posted times, so build your Halloween around them. The billing runs Gabriella Rose first, Kameron Marlowe in direct support, then Ella as the headliner. ## Where to sit and what to expect

Grand Casino Arena is a climate-controlled indoor arena in downtown Saint Paul (199 W Kellogg Blvd), so weather won't be a factor — leave the poncho at home. It's a large room; the floor and lower bowl get you closest, but the upper levels still have clean sightlines to the stage. If you're coming in costume, give yourself extra time at the doors. Full section details are on the [Saint Paul set-times and seating page](/tour/saint-paul-grand-casino-arena-2026-10-31/set-times).
