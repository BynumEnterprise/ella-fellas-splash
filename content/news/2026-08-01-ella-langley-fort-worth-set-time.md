The Texas run ends where Texas runs should: Fort Worth. **Saturday, August 15, 2026** at Dickies Arena is the Ella Langley Fort Worth show — the third arena in three nights and the last date of the original Dandelion Tour routing before the new fall leg picks up five days later. If you're heading to Montgomery Street that night, here's the set time the venue has actually posted, who's on the bill, and what to sort out before you go.

## What time does Ella Langley go on in Fort Worth?

Dickies Arena has posted the official schedule on its [event page](https://dickiesarena.com/event/2026-08-15-ella-langley/):

- **6:00 PM CT** — doors open
- **7:00 PM CT** — show starts

The bill is **Kaitlin Butts** and **Gabriella Rose** supporting, with Ella headlining. The venue hasn't published a per-act running order, so 7:00 PM is the honest, sourced answer — that's when the first act takes the stage. On recent three-act Dandelion nights Ella has closed after both openers, which puts her set well into the evening, but we're not going to invent a minute the venue hasn't printed. If a full schedule appears closer to the date, this page and the [Fort Worth set-times page](/tour/fort-worth-dickies-arena-2026-08-15/set-times) get the real numbers.

Show up for the openers anyway. Kaitlin Butts has been earning rooms all tour in the direct-support slot, and Gabriella Rose is the kind of opener you'll want to say you caught early.

## The last night of the original run

This date matters a little more than a normal tour stop. When ["The Dandelion Tour"](/tour) was announced, Fort Worth was the closer — sixteen dates wrapping August 15. Then the extension happened: [Pollstar reported](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/06/22/ella-langley-extends-the-dandelion-tour-with-21-new-dates/) 21 new dates starting August 20 in Green Bay and running through Halloween in Saint Paul. So Fort Worth is now the finale of the first chapter — the crowd that bought these tickets thought they were getting the tour closer, and we'd bet the show treats it like one.

Expect the songs everybody came for — "[Choosin' Texas](/songs/choosin-texas)" on its third straight Texas night, "[you look like you love me](/songs/you-look-like-you-love-me)," "[Be Her](/songs/be-her)" — alongside the deeper *Dandelion* cuts. The actual setlist goes up on the [Fort Worth setlist page](/tour/fort-worth-dickies-arena-2026-08-15/setlist) within a couple of hours of the encore.

## Dickies Arena notes

Dickies sits at 1911 Montgomery St in the Will Rogers Memorial Center district, west of downtown — a genuinely nice room, roughly fourteen thousand for a concert, and indoors, which in Texas in mid-August is not a small thing. The venue runs its own [parking and directions page](https://dickiesarena.com/directions-and-parking/); lots around the Will Rogers campus fill early on Saturday event nights, so sort parking before you leave.
