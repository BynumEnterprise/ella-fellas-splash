**Short answer:** Ella Langley is one of three headliners at **Sand In My Boots 2027**, Morgan Wallen's beach festival in **Gulf Shores, Alabama, May 21-23, 2027**, billed alongside Wallen and Kings of Leon. Passes go on sale **Friday, November 6 at 10 a.m. CT**, and you have to [register on the festival site](https://sandinmybootsfest.com/) first to get access. Her exact day hasn't been announced yet.

The lineup dropped Monday, November 2. Wallen curated the 33-act bill with AEG Presents, [per JamBase](https://www.jambase.com/article/sand-in-my-boots-2027-lineup-morgan-wallen), and the festival is back after skipping 2026. Langley played the first edition in 2025. This time her name sits right under Wallen's on the poster.

## Sand In My Boots 2027 at a glance

- **Dates:** Friday-Sunday, May 21-23, 2027
- **Where:** The festival site on the public beach at 101 E Beach Blvd, Gulf Shores, AL 36542 (the old Hangout Fest grounds)
- **Headliners:** Morgan Wallen, Kings of Leon, Ella Langley
- **Ella's day:** Not announced. The full three-day schedule is expected closer to the festival, [The Bama Buzz reports](https://thebamabuzz.com/ella-langley-kings-of-leon-headlining-2027-sand-in-my-boots-festival/), so plan on a full-weekend pass for now
- **Local presale:** Thursday, November 5 at 1 p.m., per The Bama Buzz, for buyers with a billing ZIP code in the Mobile and Baldwin County, Alabama, and Pensacola, Florida areas
- **Public on-sale:** Friday, November 6 at 10 a.m. CT, after registering at sandinmybootsfest.com

We've added it to her [2027 tour dates](/guides/ella-langley-2027-tour) and built a [Gulf Shores show page](/tour/gulf-shores-sand-in-my-boots-2027-05-21). It's listed under May 21, the first day, until the festival says which day she plays.

## How much are Sand In My Boots 2027 passes?

The festival posts all-in prices: what you see already includes taxes and the beach restoration fee, with shipping added at checkout. These are weekend passes (GA is sold as a 3-day pass), and no single-day tickets are listed. Here are the prices on the [official passes page](https://sandinmybootsfest.com/passes) as of November 3:

- **General Admission:** $676.87. All GA areas all weekend, swimming and beach club access. There's also a GA 4-pack and a GA + shuttle bundle.
- **Party Pit:** $1,354.87. The front-of-stage viewing area at the Main Stage, plus its own bar.
- **VIP:** $2,484.87. Dedicated viewing areas, dipping pools, complimentary food and drink, air-conditioned restrooms.
- **Super VIP:** $3,614.87. Everything in VIP, plus a dining room and a sandbar overlooking the main stage.
- **Livin' the Dream:** $7,698.90. The top tier, with its own lounge, a shuttle between stages and exclusive viewing areas.
- **Cabanas:** private beachside cabanas for up to 25 guests a day, priced separately.

You can spread the cost out. Checkout offers a 25%-down payment plan, Klarna and PayPal. There's also an optional **Pre-Game** night ($108.90), which you can only buy with a festival pass.

A few rules worth knowing before you buy. The festival is all ages, but anyone 17 or under needs a ticketed adult 21 or older with them, and kids 5 and under get in free with a pass-holding parent. It's cashless. And **there's no festival parking**: you park where you're staying or take the shuttle.

## Who else is on the Sand In My Boots 2027 lineup?

After the three headliners, the bill runs Kygo, Empire of the Sun, Nelly, Flo Rida, Gavin Adcock, Koe Wetzel, Cole Swindell, Third Eye Blind, Midland, Hudson Westbrook, Gucci Mane, Vincent Mason, Rick Ross, Corey Kent, Avery Anna, Kameron Marlowe, Zach John King, Juicy J, Girl Talk, Ying Yang Twins, Waylon Wyatt, Project Pat, Blake Whiten, Corey Smith, Carter Faith, Jason Scott & The High Heat, Kashus Culpepper, Jacob Hackworth, Kenny Whitmire and Phil Kane.

Ella fans will know a lot of those names. Kameron Marlowe opened the fall leg of the [Dandelion Tour](/guides/ella-langley-fall-tour-2026) and kept coming out to sing "Strangers" with her. Koe Wetzel is her partner on ["Jaded"](/songs/jaded). Gavin Adcock, Jason Scott & The High Heat, Hudson Westbrook, Blake Whiten and Zach John King all shared bills with her on Wallen's 2026 stadium dates, where Ella was direct support. And she and Wallen have the CMA-nominated duet ["I Can't Love You Anymore"](/songs/i-cant-love-you-anymore).

"One thing I'm looking forward to the most about bringing Sand In My Boots Festival back is being able to have new concepts on stage and trying to elevate the experience overall for the fans, myself and other artists on the bill," Wallen said in the announcement. "I think it's going to be really top-notch and we're going to try to do it even better this time."

## Where to stay for Sand In My Boots

Book your stay early. It's a beach town on a festival weekend, and with no on-site parking you'll want to be close enough to walk or catch the shuttle. The festival sells official **hotel packages** (pass plus room) on its own site. If you'd rather book your own place, the [Gulf Shores show page](/tour/gulf-shores-sand-in-my-boots-2027-05-21) has vacation rentals and hotels near the festival grounds, already set to the weekend.
