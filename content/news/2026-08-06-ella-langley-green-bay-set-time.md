Ella Langley brings The Dandelion Tour to the **Resch Center in Green Bay on Thursday, August 20, 2026** — an arena show right in the shadow of Lambeau Field. It's a date she added after most of the tour had already sold out, which makes it a little unusual: unlike the Texas run the week before, you can still walk up to this one at face value. Here's the timing and the plan.

## Who plays when

The Resch Center has posted the number that matters, and it's the one to build your night around:

- **7:00 PM CT** — show starts (the venue's own posted time)
- **6:00 PM CT** — doors open

The bill, per the venue's event page, is **Laci Kaye Booth** first, then **ERNEST** in the direct-support slot, then **Ella Langley**.

We're not going to invent a minute for when Ella walks out. The Resch Center has published the show time and doors, but not a per-act running order, so 7:00 PM is the honest, sourced answer — the first opener goes on at the time printed on your ticket, and on recent three-act Dandelion nights Ella has closed the evening after both openers. If the venue posts a full schedule closer to the date, this page and the [set-times page](/tour/green-bay-resch-center-2026-08-20/set-times) get the real times.

Do get there for the openers. ERNEST is not a name you skip — he's one of Nashville's most-cut songwriters with a hit catalog of his own, and slotting him as direct support means the room will already be warm by the time Ella takes over. Laci Kaye Booth opens the night and is worth catching from the first song.

## Resch Center notes

This is an easy one to plan for: indoor arena, climate controlled, no weather to worry about. It sits in the Resch Complex right across from Lambeau Field, so parking is straightforward but cashless — figure **$15 in the Lambeau Field lot or $25 in the NV Technologies lot** across from the arena. Bags are limited to **8.5" x 11"** and smaller moves through security faster, so travel light.

Two things specific to this show worth knowing before you buy: tickets have **delayed mobile delivery** (the venue sends them out roughly two weeks ahead, so don't panic if they're not in your wallet right away), and there's a **six-ticket-per-household limit**.

For where to sit — lower bowl versus the upper level, and which sections get the best sound — see the [Green Bay set-times and where-to-sit page](/tour/green-bay-resch-center-2026-08-20/set-times).
