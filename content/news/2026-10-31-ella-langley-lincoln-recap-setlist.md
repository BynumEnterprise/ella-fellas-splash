Ella Langley pulled into Lincoln on Thursday night, October 30, for the next-to-last stop of the Dandelion Tour, filling Pinnacle Bank Arena with Gabriella Rose and Kameron Marlowe along for the ride as openers. One night left after this one, and you could feel the tour winding down to its final swing.

Here's the honest part: as of this morning, nobody had posted the Lincoln setlist to setlist.fm yet, and we haven't tracked down a full account of the night from anyone who was in the room. We're not going to make one up. What we can tell you is exactly what Ella's been playing on this leg, because the set has barely moved from city to city, and we'll drop in the confirmed Lincoln order the second it surfaces. If you were there, scroll down and help us fill it in.

## The set Ella's been running

Just the night before in Des Moines she played 22 songs, and that running order has been remarkably steady across the fall dates. Until Lincoln's exact set is confirmed, this is your best guide to what she almost certainly played:

1. [Hell at Night](/songs/hell-at-night) / Froggy Went A-Courtin' (BigXthaPlug cover)
2. Dandelion
3. I Gotta Quit
4. Bottom of Your Boots
5. I Can't Love You Anymore
6. Here for the Party (Gretchen Wilson cover)
7. Girl You're Taking Home
8. 20-20
9. You and Me Time
10. Last Call for Us
11. Broken
12. Somethin' Simple
13. Butterfly Season
14. Strangers (Kameron Marlowe cover)
15. Speaking Terms
16. Be Her
17. You Look Like You Love Me
18. Loving Life Again
19. Choosin' Texas
20. Weren't for the Wind
21. Wish I Didn't Know Now (Toby Keith cover)
22. That's Why We Fight

That's the Des Moines set, note for note. Lincoln may have shuffled a song or swapped the encore, so treat it as the map, not the territory, until we confirm.

## The moments to watch for

If Lincoln ran true to form, there was plenty to love. That opener is a monster, Ella flipping her own "Hell at Night" into the "Froggy Went A-Courtin'" hook she cut with BigXthaPlug. Gabriella Rose has been coming back out for "Butterfly Season," and Kameron Marlowe has been joining her for "Strangers" before she sends everyone home. The Toby Keith cover, "Wish I Didn't Know Now," has been a quiet highlight late in the set. Whether the guests came out in Lincoln the same way, we'll update once we hear from folks who were there.
