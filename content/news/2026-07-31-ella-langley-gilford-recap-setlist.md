One year ago Ella Langley walked onto the Gilford stage as Riley Green's opener with a 30-minute slot. Thursday night she came back to BankNH Pavilion with her name at the top of the ticket and 9,500 people in the seats — a sold-out show, and the only New England date she gave The Dandelion Tour this year. If you wanted to see her in this corner of the country in 2026, last night in Gilford was it.

We love a full-circle show, and this was a good one. Resale seats were going for north of $700 by showtime, which tells you everything about where this year has taken her.

## How the night ran

The venue had the lineup posted straight from the source: Whitney Doucet got things going on the Hazy Little Stage at 5:00, Laci Kaye Booth opened the main stage at 7:10, Kameron Marlowe hit at 7:50, and Ella took the stage at 9:00. Doors at 5.

Marlowe and Booth is a strong undercard for an amphitheater night — Marlowe's been drawing his own headline crowds all year, and Booth's set has been winning over early arrivals the whole tour.

## The setlist

The fan-logged setlist from Gilford hasn't been posted yet — we'll update this page once it lands. But her Dandelion Tour set has been remarkably steady all summer. Here's what she played at her most recent logged headline show (Wilmington, NC, June 26, per setlist.fm), and Gilford almost certainly ran close to it:

1. Dandelion
2. I Gotta Quit
3. Bottom of Your Boots
4. Country Boy's Dream Girl
5. girl you're taking home
6. 20-20
7. You & Me Time
8. Last Call for Us
9. Here for the Party (Gretchen Wilson cover)
10. Broken
11. Somethin' Simple
12. Butterfly Season
13. Speaking Terms (acoustic)
14. Be Her
15. you look like you love me
16. Loving Life Again
17. [Choosin' Texas](/songs/choosin-texas)
18. weren't for the wind

That back stretch — "you look like you love me" into "Loving Life Again" into "Choosin' Texas" into "weren't for the wind" — has been closing shows all tour, and there's not a wasted minute in it.
