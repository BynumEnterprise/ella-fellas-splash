If you've seen "the Dandelion Tour finale" attached to Ella Langley's Fort Worth show and assumed this weekend is your last shot at seeing her in 2026, you're reading it the way the early headlines wrote it — and they're out of date. Fort Worth's Dickies Arena on August 15 does close a chapter of the Dandelion Tour, but the Dandelion Tour is not over after Fort Worth. Ella has a whole second leg booked that opens five days later and runs all the way to Halloween, so nobody should be treating Saturday like the final curtain.

## The short answer

No. August 15 in Fort Worth is the last night of the *first* run of Dandelion Tour arena dates — the leg that was originally announced with Fort Worth as its capper, which is why so much of the press called it the "finale." Since then Ella has added a second leg. It starts **August 20 in Green Bay** and ends **October 31 in St. Paul**, with a stretch of the fall's most wanted rooms in between. If you can't get to Texas this weekend, you have plenty of runway.

## Why Fort Worth got called "the finale"

When the Dandelion Tour was first laid out, it was a tight 16-city arena run that wrapped in Texas, and Ella pointed to Fort Worth on purpose — she'd played Billy Bob's in that same city on the way up, and closing her first headlining arena tour at Dickies Arena was the full-circle beat every outlet wanted to write. That's a real story, and Fort Worth genuinely is the end of that original itinerary. The word "finale" just stuck before the calendar changed.

The three Texas nights that close the leg all happen this week: **Austin at Moody Center on August 13**, **Corpus Christi on August 14**, and **Fort Worth at Dickies Arena on August 15** (venue-posted doors 6:00 PM, show 7:00 PM). Kaitlin Butts and Gabriella Rose open all three. We've got the room-by-room details on the [Austin set time page](/news/2026-07-30-ella-langley-austin-set-time) and the [Fort Worth set time page](/news/2026-08-01-ella-langley-fort-worth-set-time), and every date sits on the [Ella Langley tour hub](/tour).

## What actually comes next: the fall leg

In June, Ella extended the Dandelion Tour with 21 new dates, and that's the part that changes the "is it over" question. The second leg picks up **August 20 at the Resch Center in Green Bay** and keeps rolling through the fall — including a night at **Red Rocks Amphitheatre on October 7** and **two shows at the Greek Theatre in Los Angeles (October 13 and 14)** — before it finishes on **October 31 at Grand Casino Arena in St. Paul**. Support across the fall run rotates through ERNEST, Kameron Marlowe, Laci Kaye Booth, and Gabriella Rose depending on the city.

So the honest map looks like this: the arena leg ends in Fort Worth on August 15, there's a five-day breather, and then the tour is right back at it. If your city is Green Bay, North Little Rock, Auburn, Newark, Jacksonville, Knoxville, Columbus, Tulsa, Lubbock, Des Moines, Lincoln, or one of the marquee stops at Red Rocks or the Greek, your Dandelion night is still ahead of you. The [Green Bay set time page](/news/2026-08-06-ella-langley-green-bay-set-time) is the first one up, and if you're still deciding which era of the show you're walking into, our [breakdown of what "Dandelion" means](/news/2026-08-09-ella-langley-dandelion-meaning) is a good primer on the album these nights are built around.
