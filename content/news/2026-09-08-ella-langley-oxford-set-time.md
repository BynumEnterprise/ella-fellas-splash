Ella Langley brings The Dandelion Tour to the **Sandy and John Black Pavilion at Ole Miss in Oxford on Thursday, September 17, 2026** — landing in town right as Oxford fills up for Ole Miss-LSU that weekend. If you're in for the football, this is the way to open the trip. Here's the timing and the plan.

## Who plays when

The ticketed start time is **7:00 PM**, with doors about an hour before — that's the number to build your night around.

The bill is **Laci Kaye Booth** first, then **Kameron Marlowe** in the direct-support slot, then **Ella Langley**.

We won't guess a minute for when Ella walks out. The listed 7:00 PM start is the reliable time; the first opener goes on around then, and on recent three-act Dandelion nights Ella closes after both openers. If the Pavilion posts a full per-act running order closer to the date, this page and the [set-times page](/tour/oxford-the-pavilion-ole-miss-2026-09-17/set-times) will carry the real times.

Worth getting in early: **Kameron Marlowe** is a headliner in his own right — "Giving You Up," "Tennessee Orange" — so the direct-support slot is a full set, not a warm-up. **Laci Kaye Booth** opens the night.

## Pavilion at Ole Miss notes

It's an indoor arena on campus, climate controlled, so there's no weather to worry about — but a football weekend means Oxford traffic and parking will be heavier than a normal Thursday. Give yourself extra time getting in, follow the campus event-parking signage, and screenshot your mobile ticket before you're in a slow line. The room runs cool once it's full; a light layer helps.
