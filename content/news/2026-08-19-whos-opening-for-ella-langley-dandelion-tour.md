If you've got a ticket to one of the fall shows and you're wondering who's opening for Ella Langley on the Dandelion Tour, the honest answer is: it depends on the night. The second leg runs on a rotating support lineup, so the name you'll see when doors open in Green Bay isn't the same one playing Red Rocks or the Greek. Here's who's in the rotation, who's confirmed for the leg-opening show, and how the fall lineup differs from the arena run everybody just watched wrap in Fort Worth.

## The short answer

The Dandelion Tour's second leg opens **August 20 at the Resch Center in Green Bay, Wisconsin**, and runs all the way to a Halloween-night finale at Grand Casino Arena in Saint Paul — 21 new dates in all, including Red Rocks in Morrison, Colorado and two nights at the Greek Theatre in Los Angeles. Across those shows, opening duties rotate between **Kaitlin Butts, Kameron Marlowe, Dylan Marlowe, Ernest, Laci Kaye Booth, and Gabriella Rose**, so which one you catch comes down to the date on your ticket.

For the **Green Bay opener specifically**, the venue has **Ernest and Laci Kaye Booth** on the bill with Ella. That's your leg-one, night-one pairing.

## Who's who in the rotation

A few of these names carry over from the arena leg, and a couple are new. If you never caught the first run, here's the quick read on who's worth being in your seat for:

- **Kaitlin Butts** — red-dirt Oklahoma country with an edge, writes everything herself. If you love the Ella songs that are actually about something, she'll land the same way.
- **Kameron Marlowe** — a smoky, blue-collar baritone with a genuinely tight touring band. One of the sets worth showing up at doors for.
- **Dylan Marlowe** — Kameron's younger brother, radio-leaning modern country; a carryover from the arena leg.
- **Ernest** — the marquee new name for the fall. He's a heavyweight songwriter (a hand in a stack of recent country hits) stepping into the opener rotation.
- **Laci Kaye Booth** — a soulful, throwback voice who's been in Ella's orbit for a while; she's on the Green Bay bill.
- **Gabriella Rose** — one of the newer artists Ella has been putting in front of arena crowds all year.

## Who's new for the fall

If you followed the arena leg, most of this bench will look familiar. Kaitlin Butts, Kameron Marlowe, Dylan Marlowe, Laci Kaye Booth, and Gabriella Rose all rotated through the spring and summer shows, and they're all back for the fall. The one genuinely new name is **Ernest** — the biggest songwriter of the group and the marquee addition for the second leg.

What changes more than the roster is the setting. The arena leg was built almost entirely around big indoor buildings. The fall run mixes in the kind of rooms fans circle on a calendar: Red Rocks in the Colorado foothills, two nights at the open-air Greek Theatre in Los Angeles, and a Halloween-night close in Saint Paul. Same deep opening bill, better backdrops.
