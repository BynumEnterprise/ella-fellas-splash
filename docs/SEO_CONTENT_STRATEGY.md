## 0. Strategic North Star

**We will not win "Ella Fellas" as a branded shopping term head-on.** The official merch store (ellalangley.us), Amazon t-shirt listings, TeePublic, Etsy, and TikTok Shop all compete there with stronger entity authority. Google's intent for that query is transactional/navigational; we are informational.

**Our winning lane: own the informational + fan-intent universe around Ella Langley.** Tour prep, song meanings, setlist tracking, ticket guidance, comparisons, and concert outfit ideas generate high organic volume at far lower competition — and build topical authority that lifts our brand term passively. Every affiliate click on tickets or Amazon gear is a revenue event.

**One-line positioning:** *"The unofficial Ella Langley superfan HQ — where to find tickets, decode her songs, and show up to the show ready."*

| About/bio spoke | Ella Langley [Topic]: [What Fans Want to Know] | Ella Langley Biography: From Alabama to Country's #1 |

**Hard rules:**
- 50-60 characters. Use a character counter — Google truncates at ~60.
- Include "Ella Langley" in every title tag. It is the primary entity.
- Never use ALL CAPS.
- One primary keyword per title.
- Every title must be unique across the site.

---

### 4B. Meta Description Rules

**Formula:** [What the page delivers] + [One differentiating detail] + [Soft CTA]. Aim for 130-155 characters.

**Examples by type:**

- Song meaning: "We break down 'Choosin' Texas' — Ella Langley's Miranda Lambert co-write that made Hot 100 history. The real story behind the lyrics, decoded."
- City ticket: "Ella Langley comes to Dickies Arena in Fort Worth on Aug 15. Here's where to buy tickets, best seats, parking, and what to expect."
- Concert prep: "High-waisted denim, concho belts, vintage western. Our concert outfit guide for the Dandelion Tour — with Amazon links to every piece."
- Comparison: "Both top the charts. Both are real. Here's how Ella Langley and Megan Moroney differ — and why Nashville needs both."

**Hard rules:**
- Never duplicate meta descriptions.
- Do not use "fans rejoice," "broke the internet," or other cliches (brand voice rule).
- Include a natural-language keyword variation of the title tag — do not just repeat the title.
- Write for the reader. Google rewrites mechanical metas.

### 4D. Internal Linking Rules

- Every article must link to its parent hub page (above the fold if possible, minimum once).
- Every article must link to at least 2 related spokes in the same hub.
- Song meaning pages always link to: the album hub, 2 other songs from the same album, and the tour page.
- Tour/city pages always link to: the tour hub, the setlist page, and the concert prep guide.
- The news feed (/news) must tag each post and link to the relevant hub.
- Do not orphan pages — every published URL must receive at least one internal link from a published page.
- Anchor text: descriptive, natural, keyword-adjacent. Not "click here."
  - Good: "our full Dandelion tour setlist tracker"
  - Bad: "here" / "this page" / bare URL

### 4E-2. Photos of Ella — licensing and attribution (MANDATORY, added 2026-07-30)

Owner mandate: **every news post carries at least one photo of Ella.** Walls of text don't get read. Longer posts should carry a second photo where it earns its place in the argument.

**The only legal source of images is `lib/photos.ts`.** Two kinds of entry live there:

- `rights: "owned"` — photos the owner supplies and holds full rights to. He drops them in `.work/ella-photos/` and confirmed (2026-07-30) he will only ever hand over images he owns. These render with a caption and an "Ella Fellas photo" line.
- `rights: "cc"` — Creative Commons / public-domain files (currently CC BY and CC BY-SA from Wikimedia Commons), rendered with photographer + source + license, all linked.

`components/PhotoFigure.tsx` handles both, so credit can never be silently dropped. **Check `.work/ella-photos/` for new drops before going hunting on Commons** — anything new in there is cleared for use and is better quality than what Commons has.

How to use it:

- Frontmatter: `heroPhoto: "<id>"` and optionally `heroPhotoCaption: "..."` for a context-specific caption. The lead photo is also used as the OpenGraph/Twitter image and the `NewsArticle` schema `image`.
- Inside the body: `<Figure photo="<id>" caption="..." />`. Works in news posts and guides.
- Omitting `heroPhoto` is safe — a photo is auto-assigned deterministically per slug so no post is ever image-less — but an explicitly chosen photo is always better.

**Never publish:** press/wire/Getty/AP photos, publicity stills, album art we don't have rights to, screenshots of her Instagram/TikTok/X, or fan photos lifted from social. This site is a commercial affiliate property; "it's on the internet" is not a license.

**Adding to the pool** (do this whenever you find a new one — it keeps the blog from repeating itself):

1. Confirm the license on the Wikimedia Commons file page is CC BY, CC BY-SA, or PD. `Category:Ella Langley` on Commons grows over time; check every week or two.
2. Save a web-sized copy (max 1400px wide, q82 progressive JPEG) into `public/images/ella/` with a descriptive filename.
3. Add a registry entry with real `width`/`height`, unique descriptive `alt` (per 4E), a caption naming the **actual venue and date**, `photographer`, `sourceUrl` (the Commons file page), `license`, `licenseUrl`.

**Captions must be honest.** The photo is usually not from the show or event the post covers, so the caption always states the real venue and date. Never imply we shot it, never imply it's from the event being written about.

## 5. Quality Bar Checklist

Every piece of content published to EllaFellas.com must pass all of these before going live.

### Research & Accuracy
- [ ] All factual claims (chart positions, dates, prices, quotes) are sourced from a named publication or official source — not inferred or hallucinated.
- [ ] Tour dates and venue names are verified against Ticketmaster or official artist site on day of publish.
- [ ] All Amazon ASINs in the /shop are verified live and point to the correct in-stock product. Zero search fallbacks. (Per zero-amazon-search-policy.)
- [ ] If covering a recent milestone (chart position, award), the claim includes the date it was accurate and is marked for update.
- [ ] No stats, quotes, or "facts" that cannot be traced to a primary or reputable secondary source.

### Content Depth
- [ ] The article answers the full question implied by its target keyword — not a partial answer padded with filler.
- [ ] Minimum useful length: song meaning pages 700+ words; guides 900+ words; city/ticket pages 500+ words; news posts 300+ words. No minimum is a good post; the minimum is just the floor for passing.
- [ ] Does this article add something a fan could not get from the first 3 Google results? If not, rewrite or spike it.
- [ ] No AI-tell language: "delve into," "it's worth noting," "in today's landscape," "a testament to," "let's explore." Read aloud. If it sounds like a press release, fix it.

### Voice & Brand
- [ ] Written in first-person plural ("we") unless a clearly personal guide post.
- [ ] Reads like a knowledgeable country music fan wrote it — enthusiastic, direct, specific — not a music blog or analyst memo.
- [ ] Zero instances of cliched phrases: "fans rejoice," "dropped," "broke the internet," "is living her best life," "stans," "slaps," "banger." Check before publish.
- [ ] Specific details over vague superlatives. "Choosin' Texas spent 21 weeks at No. 1 on Hot Country Songs" not "Choosin' Texas was hugely successful."

### SEO Technical
- [ ] Title tag 50-60 characters, unique across site, contains primary keyword.
- [ ] Meta description 130-155 characters, unique, reads naturally, not a keyword list.
- [ ] H1 is present and unique (not identical to title tag).
- [ ] At least 2 internal links to related hub or spoke pages.
- [ ] All images have descriptive, unique alt text.
- [ ] Target keyword appears in: H1, first 100 words of body, at least one H2, and naturally 2-4 times in body.
- [ ] Page links to its parent hub.
- [ ] Appropriate schema type implemented.
- [ ] No duplicate content from another page on the site.

### Affiliate & Commerce
- [ ] All affiliate links are tagged (ellafellas-20 for Amazon; correct affiliate ID for ticket platforms).
- [ ] Affiliate disclosure present on any page with affiliate links (FTC requirement — "Some links on this page are affiliate links — we may earn a small commission at no cost to you.").
- [ ] Ticket links open in new tab; verified that landing page is not a 404.
- [ ] No price claims in body text that could become inaccurate — use "check current price" language, or put prices in schema only.

### Freshness Flag
- [ ] Does this content have a shelf life? If yes, add it to the "pages to review" schedule:
  - Tour pages: review/update weekly during active tour.
  - Chart records: update within 24h of a new milestone.
  - Setlist pages: update within 12h of each show night.
  - Ticket pages: review every 2 weeks for sold-out status.
  - Song meaning pages: evergreen — quarterly check only.
