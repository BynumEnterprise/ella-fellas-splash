We keep thinking this song has to slow down, and every Tuesday the chart tells us otherwise. On the Billboard Hot 100 dated August 22, ["Choosin' Texas"](/songs/choosin-texas) holds at No. 1 for an 18th week — a run that started back in February and still has not blinked, [as Billboard confirmed](https://www.billboard.com/lists/ella-langley-choosin-texas-number-1-hot-100-18-week/).

Here is the part that should make every Ella fan sit up: 18 weeks puts her one single week away from the longest No. 1 run in Hot 100 history for a non-holiday song. That all-time mark is 19 weeks, shared by Lil Nas X's "Old Town Road" and Shaboozey's "A Bar Song (Tipsy)." If "Choosin' Texas" stays put next week, an Alabama girl's story ties two of the biggest crossover records of the last decade.

## The Records Already Behind Her

The 18th week isn't the first history she's made this month. When the song reached its 17th week on the August 15 chart, it passed Mariah Carey and Boyz II Men's "One Sweet Day," the 16-week juggernaut that stood as a benchmark for nearly 30 years. Variety noted that the milestone left "Choosin' Texas" [alone in fourth place](https://variety.com/2026/music/news/ella-langley-choosin-texas-17th-week-hot-100-1236831734/) among the longest No. 1 streaks ever. A week later she's climbed even higher on that list.

For context, the only reign that still tops hers outright over a full calendar is Mariah Carey's "All I Want for Christmas Is You," and that one needed multiple holiday seasons to bank its total. What Langley has done, she's done in one continuous stretch, on a country single, in a single year.

## Why It Keeps Holding

This isn't a streaming fluke or a radio-only story. The Hot 100 weighs streams, airplay and sales together, and "Choosin' Texas" keeps showing up across all three while the rest of her catalog climbs behind it, [per Holler](https://holler.country/news/breaking/ella-langleys-choosin-texas-spends-18th-week-at-billboard-no-1/). We wrote earlier this month about the week [country swept the entire Hot 100 top five](/news/2026-08-08-ella-langley-country-hot-100-top-5-sweep) — this is the same song, still doing the heavy lifting at the very top.
