The Dandelion Tour is Ella Langley's 2026 headlining tour. It opened May 7 in Toledo and runs through October 31 in Saint Paul, with arenas, amphitheaters and a few festival stops along the way. If you're going, this is the guide for getting there, getting in, getting through the show, and getting home without missing anything.

## Before the show — three weeks out

**Lock the ticket.** Resale prices move around, and waiting for a last-minute drop is a gamble. If you know you're going, buy when you decide.

For resale seats, browse TicketNetwork — every listing shows the all-in price up front and is backed by a buyer guarantee, so you know exactly what you're paying before you commit.

**Check the venue's bag policy.** Many arenas are clear-bag only, and amphitheater rules differ from venue to venue. Check your venue's own bag policy before you go.

**Book the hotel within 48 hours of buying the ticket.** Hotels near these venues fill up fast for show nights. Staying within walking distance of the venue saves you from post-show rideshare surge pricing.

## Day-of — the timing

Doors and start times vary by venue, so check your ticket and our [set times page](/set-times) for your show. Most nights have two opening acts before Ella. The rotation has included Kameron Marlowe, Dylan Marlowe, Kaitlin Butts, Laci Kaye Booth, Gabriella Rose, ERNEST and Muscadine Bloodline, depending on the date. (Kameron and Dylan Marlowe are not related, despite the shared last name.) Our [opening acts guide](/guides/opening-acts-explained) covers who they are.

Eat before doors if you want to catch the first opener.

## The setlist preview

Based on the setlists we have logged from July through October 2026, expect roughly 18 to 23 songs. The pattern has been consistent:

1. **Opens** with "Dandelion," the title track, at every show we have logged
2. **Dandelion album cuts** make up much of the set ("I Gotta Quit," "Bottom of Your Boots," "You & Me Time," "Somethin' Simple," "Butterfly Season" and more), alongside "Here for the Party" (the Gretchen Wilson song) and "That's Why We Fight"
3. **The big singles** come late: "Be Her," "you look like you love me," "Loving Life Again" and "Choosin' Texas"
4. **Main set usually closes** with "weren't for the wind"
5. **Encore** varies by night; on the fall run it has often included "Wish I Didn't Know Now" (her Toby Keith cover) and "That's Why We Fight"

Full song-by-song lists for each show are on our [setlists page](/setlists).

## Where to sit, what to skip

If you're buying tickets fresh, here's the price/value breakdown for the Dandelion Tour arenas:

- **Floor (rows 1-15):** Worth the premium if you can swing it. The sound mix is best here and Ella plays *to* the floor.
- **Floor (rows 16+):** Mid-tier price, but you're far enough back that the sound suffers and the view requires the screens.
- **Lower bowl (sides, sections 100-115):** The sweet spot. Better sound than back-floor, half the price of front-floor, full venue view.
- **Upper bowl:** Cheap and surprisingly good for the visuals — the stage lighting is designed to read from up here.

Avoid sections directly behind the soundboard if your venue has them. The view of the stage is blocked or partially blocked by the lighting rig in most arena configurations.

## After the show

Most arena tours empty out within 25 minutes of lights-up. The bottleneck is parking, not the venue exit. Two strategies:

- **Stay in your seat for 15 minutes.** Watch the lights come up, finish your drink, let the lot clear. You'll get out faster than the people who rushed.
- **Pre-game the exit.** Walk out during the encore (you'll catch the bigger song from the parking lot tunnel anyway), get to your car, beat the traffic.

If you want the post-show country bar scene, every city on the tour has one worth checking out. We're building [city-by-city guides](/tour) for each stop with the actual bars locals go to — not the tourist trap honky-tonks.

- Cash or card for parking (check your venue; many lots are cashless now)
- A light layer (arenas run cold during openers, hot during headliner)
- Closed-toe shoes, or boots if that's your style — [Pinto Ranch](https://www.kqzyfj.com/click-101760569-15736262) has the western wear that holds up past show night

## What to leave at home

- Large or pro cameras (most venues ban detachable-lens cameras; check your venue's policy)
- Anything in a non-clear bag at most arenas
- Outside drinks
