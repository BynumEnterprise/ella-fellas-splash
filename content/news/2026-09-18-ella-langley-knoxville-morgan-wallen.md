If you're going to the Dandelion Tour's Knoxville stop and you've seen the chatter, here's the short version: Ella Langley plays Knoxville's Food City Center on Thursday, Sept. 24, 2026, and the only guests actually on the bill are Kameron Marlowe and Laci Kaye Booth. A Morgan Wallen cameo is a fun thing to hope for, but as of now it is a rumor, not an announcement. We'll walk through what's confirmed, why the Wallen talk started, and what a realistic night in Knoxville looks like.

## What's Actually Confirmed for Sept. 24

The date, the venue, and the supporting acts are all locked. [The University of Tennessee's official event listing](https://utsports.com/sports/2026/7/1/ella-langley-event-information) has the show at Food City Center with Kameron Marlowe and Laci Kaye Booth as the announced special guests, and [Hoodline confirmed that same lineup](https://hoodline.com/2026/09/ella-langley-s-knoxville-show-has-a-confirmed-bill-morgan-wallen-s-appearance-does-not/) while noting there was no public indication Wallen planned to join. That's the reliable expectation: Langley's headlining set plus Marlowe and Booth. Anything beyond that is speculation until the venue, the promoter, or one of the artists says otherwise.

For set-time and doors questions, we keep a running [Knoxville set-time guide](/news/2026-09-10-ella-langley-knoxville-set-time) — check it the day of the show, since these arena start times can shift.

## Why the Morgan Wallen Rumor Started

The Wallen speculation isn't coming from nowhere, and that's worth being fair about. Langley and Wallen have genuinely shared stages this year: [Billboard reported that Langley joined Wallen for "Sand in My Boots" in Nashville, then appeared with him in Tuscaloosa where they debuted their duet "I Can't Love You Anymore."](https://www.billboard.com/music/country/morgan-wallen-ella-langley-new-duet-i-cant-love-you-anymore-1236226502/) Add Wallen's deep East Tennessee ties and Knoxville is exactly the kind of hometown-adjacent room where fans start dreaming up a surprise walk-on.

But documented past collaborations tell you the two are friendly, not that a specific date is booked. Those are two different things, and the gap between them is where most of these "surprise guest" rumors live.

## The Rotating Bill Adds to the Guesswork

The other reason people keep scanning for surprise names is that this tour hasn't run one identical undercard every night. The Dandelion Tour has rotated through a group of supporting acts across the run, which is also why the Knoxville names differ from other stops. We track the full rotating lineup in our [who's opening for Ella Langley guide](/news/2026-08-19-whos-opening-for-ella-langley-dandelion-tour), and you can see the whole route on the [Ella Langley tour hub](/tour). A flexible bill makes cameos feel more plausible than they usually are — but a plausible cameo is still not a confirmed one.

## Our Honest Take

Go for the show that's actually on the ticket. A Langley headlining set with Marlowe and Booth is a strong Thursday night on its own, and if a bigger name happens to wander out for a song, that's a bonus you didn't pay for rather than the reason to buy. Treat any "Wallen is confirmed" post you see without a venue or artist announcement attached as wishful thinking.
