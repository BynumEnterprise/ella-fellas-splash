She played in the shadow of Lambeau, and she filled the room. Ella Langley brought The Dandelion Tour to the Resch Center in Green Bay on Thursday night, a date she added after most of the tour had already sold out — and it went the same way, gone at face value with the arena on resale only by the time the lights went down. If you were there, you already know what kind of night it was. If you weren't, here's what we caught.

Laci Kaye Booth opened the evening, then ERNEST took the direct-support slot before Ella. That's a stacked bill — ERNEST is one of Nashville's most-cut songwriters with a hit catalog of his own, so the room was already warm and singing by the time she walked out. Green Bay showed up for all three.

## The setlist

A quick, honest note first: as of this morning the Green Bay setlist hasn't been logged song-by-song on setlist.fm yet, and we don't publish a set we can't verify. Fan-logged setlists usually land a couple of days after the show — the moment a Green Bay-specific list is confirmed, our [setlist page for this show](/tour/green-bay-resch-center-2026-08-20/setlist) gets the real, in-order count.

What we can tell you is the backbone the Dandelion Tour has been running, night to night:

- Dandelion
- I Gotta Quit
- Here For the Party (Gretchen Wilson cover)
- Bottom Of Your Boots
- Paint The Town Blue
- Girl You're Taking Home
- 20-20
- You & Me Time

- Be Her
- You Look Like You Love Me
- Loving Life Again

Ella shuffles the middle and swaps a cover in and out from stop to stop, so treat this as the shape of the night rather than a Green Bay-verified count. If your show ran a little different, that's the tour — not our tally.

## Moments that stuck

"[Choosin' Texas](/songs/choosin-texas)" is still the emotional peak of the set, and it's the one an arena crowd throws right back at her. "[Loving Life Again](/songs/loving-life-again)" keeps growing now that it's had radio time and people know every word. And that encore hush before "[Weren't For The Wind](/songs/werent-for-the-wind)" is the one you drive home thinking about. The read on the night was simple from the floor: great venue, great lineup, and a headliner with the stage presence to own an arena.
