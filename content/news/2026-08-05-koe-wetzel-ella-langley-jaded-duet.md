We've been waiting for these two to get back in a room together, and now it's here. Ella Langley and Koe Wetzel's new duet "Jaded" is out today as part of the deluxe edition of Koe's album *The Night Champion*, and it's streaming everywhere. If you had this reunion circled on your calendar, go hit play — it lives up to the hype.

## The Reunion Fans Have Been Hoping For

If you've followed Ella from the start, you know this pairing runs deep. Their first collaboration was "That's Why We Fight," the lead single from her 2023 debut EP *Excuse the Mess* — the song that helped put her on a lot of people's radar before "You Look Like You Love Me" ever hit. Rolling Stone went so far as to call Ella [Koe's favorite duet partner](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-country/koe-wetzel-ella-langley-new-song-jaded-1235603988/), and honestly, listen to how their voices trade lines and it's hard to argue.

## What Jaded Sounds Like

It's a funky, disco-tinged two-step — a moodier, groovier lane than "That's Why We Fight." The whole song is built around a back-and-forth between two people each trying to figure out how the other got so worn down on love, circling the question at its center: "How'd you get so jaded?" It was written by Koe and Ella alongside Jordan Schmidt and ERNEST, and it lands on the expanded version of *The Night Champion*, which stacks seven new tracks on top of the original eleven. Holler has the [full lyrics and a breakdown of the meaning](https://holler.country/lyrics/jaded-by-koe-wetzel-and-ella-langley-lyrics-and-meaning/) if you want to sit with the words.

## A Ridiculous Stretch for Ella

The timing is almost unfair. "Choosin' Texas" is still parked at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 — 16 weeks and counting — and her duet with Morgan Wallen, "I Can't Love You Anymore," [just climbed to a new peak of No. 2 on Country Airplay](/news/2026-08-05-i-cant-love-you-anymore-no-2-country-airplay). Now she's got another high-profile collaboration out in the world. She is everywhere on the charts right now, and adding a Koe Wetzel record only widens the net.
