We've watched Ella climb the festival bills all summer, so seeing her name at the very top of Two Step Inn 2027 feels like the natural next step. The Georgetown, Texas festival unveiled its 2027 lineup this week, and Ella shares top billing with country royalty Brooks & Dunn.

## When and where

Two Step Inn 2027 returns to San Gabriel Park in Georgetown, Texas, on April 17-18, 2027. It's a two-day weekend built around one of the deepest country lineups of the year, and Ella anchoring it alongside Brooks & Dunn tells you exactly how far she's come since her club-show days.

## The full lineup

The festival announced 30 acts for 2027. Beyond the Ella and Brooks & Dunn headline slots, the bill features Parker McCollum, Sheryl Crow, Flatland Cavalry, Whiskey Myers, Tracy Lawrence and the Josh Abbott Band, among many others. There's also a Dolly Parton tribute billed as "We Will Always Love You," fronted by Kaitlin Butts and Carter Faith — the kind of one-night-only moment that makes a festival weekend worth the drive.

## How the ticket presale works

If you want in, the timing matters. According to Holler and TicketNews, tickets go on sale first through a presale beginning Friday, September 25 at 12:00 p.m. CT, and you sign up for presale access on the festival's official website at twostepinn.com. Fans who get through the presale get a one-hour window to grab the lowest-priced two-day general admission tickets before prices step up.

On the general pricing side, one-day tickets start around $169 and two-day packages begin near $289, per Saving Country Music. Georgetown residents already got first crack at tickets earlier in the local presale, so out-of-towners should be ready the moment the wider presale opens.
